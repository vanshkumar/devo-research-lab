## Decision

Test whether a frozen learning rule can forecast **population acquisition under a changed training policy**, even though useful individual forecasts were not established. This is a new prospective target. It does not pass, replace or lower the completed individual-forecast gate.

This decision follows actual discriminating computations, rather than an automatic model repair. The completed RNN reproduces source mean acquisition: its descriptive six-bin mean-curve error is 1.04 percentage points versus growth's 3.48 points. That observation alone is weak grounds for another study: a selected-model same-source bootstrap of its advantage spans zero, and a source-analog N=20 calculation gives only about 32% power for the existing 3.44-point model gap. These are exploratory calculations on consumed source animals, not fresh confirmation or actual external power.

A separate input/simulation-only diagnostic changes the value of the test. On four hash-selected consumed source prefixes, the frozen RNN predicts **25.34 percentage points higher accuracy under the fixed external-style policy than under adaptive training**, measured at the identical ±100% contrast. The conditional Monte Carlo SE is 0.57 points; this excludes parameter, population and laboratory uncertainty. A noise-free identical-stimulus probe gives a 23.91-point policy difference. Growth's identical probe is policy invariant. The fixed-policy RNN-versus-growth prediction differs by about 26.8 points. The diagnostic uses the existing fixed mixed-contrast trainer, not a curriculum consisting only of easy trials. This is a large, falsifiable extrapolation; its size is not evidence it is correct.

The first step is to audit that extrapolation and freeze a new population protocol. A genuinely unused source set exists: 26 animals previously excluded for mixed Parquet/legacy storage. All have indexed core fields, but exact 1,000-prefix plus 3,000-future coverage is unverified, reward volume is absent from legacy sessions, and these are previously represented laboratories. Reconstruct formats and establish inclusion from metadata before outcomes. Use this set for prospective population calibration, without new learner fitting. Only a separately frozen successful population gate can release the 20 protected external animals for this new objective. The old gate and its failure remain in the record.
