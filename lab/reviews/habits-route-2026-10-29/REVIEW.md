# Independent HABITS route review — 2026-10-29

**Decision: allow consideration of ONE bounded feasibility revision; do not promote HABITS to a scientific campaign or approve trial access through this review.** A changed-teacher forecast could test the persistent question—whether learning inferred under one curriculum predicts acquisition under another—more directly than another fit to the consumed wheel-task observations. The existing recommendation has not established that such a forecast is executable or discriminating. The question remains unresolved, the old implementation stays parked, and Pan20 stays sealed.

**Reliability:** high for the independently reconstructed directory and specific source behavior below; conditional for deployed-trainer reconstruction; no demonstrated safe biological prefix decoder. **Scientific payoff:** potentially worthwhile as a frozen quantitative transfer test, presently unestablished. A faithful teacher replay, running decoder, or replication of faster MT training would complete supporting work without answering the question.

This review owns only this directory. Its source task was independently confirmed complete and idle. It reused the pinned local sources rather than repeating candidate discovery. All line locations below refer to `sources/intervention_evidence_2026-10-29/` at publication Git revision `8778c33e1e5f1a59fa461fbaa86ed7b8d0e15afb`; all six code/config Git blob hashes were independently checked.

**1. Trainer observability: conditionally reconstructible, with material deployment ambiguities.**

The missing companion logs do not themselves make replay impossible. HABITS Methods `s4-15-1` specifies alpha=.01, momentum=0, gamma=.03, L1=0, and the correct five-level encoding [-1,-.5,0,.5,1]. These agree with the example initialization files. A complete trial history, these settings, the actual firmware, and a declared restart/parameter-change history would largely determine the teacher. The teacher's estimator update is an engineering algorithm fitted to choices; it is not an independently established biological learning rule.

**Concrete conditional witness.** `audit.py` extracts the original C++ teacher functions without editing them, compiles with source `float`, and evaluates synthetic inputs only. With live theta [-2,2,-2,0,0,0,0], accepted-update count 100, the paper's live encoding, and a correct left response to indices (0,1) following a no-response, the next update is odd. A preserved cache selects **(3,1)**; a cache reinitialized as at boot selects **(2,0)**. An independent Python score reduction agrees. In this witness, restoring the correct cached encoding while zeroing cached theta restores (3,1): the reset encoding drives this particular discrepancy. Strict row-order tie handling is preserved.

This is an admissible hidden-state witness, **not** a proof that two deployments produced identical complete observed histories, that this state is reached from the published zero initialization, that a reset occurred, or that one changed trial materially changes the scientific conclusion. The initial finite sweeps of first-update encodings and 2–200 artificial updates found no selection discrepancy; those negative checks are retained in `CHECKS.json`. Host compilation is not Teensy hardware validation.

The strongest practical uncertainty is therefore deployed policy/state provenance, especially the later-phase difference between Methods and code. A 2,000-opportunity post-habituation prefix cannot even reach the code's phase-2 threshold of **more than 2,000 valid responses**. It cannot by itself verify later policy transitions. Full past trials may constrain the missing state; arbitrary unlogged file replacements cannot be inferred uniquely from trial rows. Do not demand proof that no manual action ever occurred, but explicitly bound credible deployment variants and require conclusions robust to them. Companion-log absence alone is not a stopping theorem.

**2. Bounded access: metadata facts pass; safe decoding remains an uncompleted prerequisite.**

The independent parser uses original `habits_zip_eocd.bin` and `habits_zip_central.bin`, not the investigator's reduction. It reconstructs 1,095 entries, central-directory offset **1,815,960,334**, directory size **217,903**, and total archive length **1,816,178,259** bytes. It matches both saved indexes entry for entry. The working-memory subtree contains exactly seven MT/trained, five Random/trained and three Random/failed `Trial.txt` files, with no other non-directory files. All fifteen are method 9; compressed total **257,833,447** bytes, fourteen over 10 MB; expanded range **31,585,841–102,688,112** bytes. CRCs, offsets, flags and hashes are in `CHECKS.json`. This checks directory integrity/consistency, not the fifteen payload CRCs.

The local synthetic method-9 ZIP is 20,152 bytes and contains only invented text. Python `zipfile` raises `NotImplementedError`; installed `bsdtar` rejects method 9 and returns zero payload bytes. The synthetic stream uses stored blocks shared with Deflate64; it does not exercise the full codec. No decoder was installed and no biological ZIP local header or payload was requested.

A bounded reader is plausible in principle: verified range access and member offsets allow capped compressed-prefix retrieval, and a decoder designed to suspend at an output limit can stop at the authorized row boundary. Large member size does not by itself force a whole-member download. **No such reader has been demonstrated here.** A future proof must exercise genuine extended-length/distance and dynamic-Huffman cases, truncated/corrupt streams, exact row stops, and both compressed and expanded hard caps. A process that emits a few lines after internally inflating later rows fails the exposure requirement. HTTP/header buffering, inflater read-ahead and decoded bytes must be accounted separately. Prefix acquisition cannot certify a whole-member CRC without consuming the rest; use version/ETag, header consistency and prefix hashes without claiming full-file validation.

**3. Scientific discrimination: possible contribution, substantial risk of another narrow replication.**

[Bak et al. 2016](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2016/file/7fec306d1e665bc9c748b5d2b99a6e97-Paper.pdf), §§4–6 (pp.5–8), already infers a policy-gradient learning component from rat training histories and develops simulated adaptive optimization. Its discussion identifies motivation and mismatch of the psychometric/learning models as practical limitations. [HABITS](https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.104833), Results `s2-4` and Fig5B–E, already demonstrates faster real-mouse MT training, easy-to-hard stimulus selection and higher served-trial accuracy. Reproducing these findings, or running that teacher with an assumed learner, does not supply the missing scientific advance.

A useful additional result would be a **source-only frozen learning process that predicts quantitatively withheld raw acquisition trajectories under another teacher**, beyond models that explain accuracy through time, stimulus difficulty, history and the teacher itself. This would support operational transfer in this task/cohort, not identify a unique biological update rule or establish general transport across laboratories. Published results and criterion identities already inform the study; call it summary-informed retrospective prediction throughout.

The alternatives must share the teacher and observation rules: (a) a source-inferred reward/history-dependent process; (b) a sensory/difficulty-aware exposure-growth model; and (c) a frozen-prefix response policy with the teacher still updating its own estimator. The third control can show apparent learning caused only by easier served trials. An additional reward/order-insensitive transition control must preserve the teacher's actual response/feedback interface. Using the teacher's online classifier update as the animal's learning law would conflate the two systems.

There is an exact scoring caution. For observed history H, inputs S and outcomes O, a known common teacher factors the joint sequence probability into products of `P_teacher(S_t | H_(t-1)) P_learner(O_t | S_t,H_(t-1))`. The common teacher factors cancel in a comparison of one-step joint log scores on the same observed history. Such a score alone is not new autonomous validation. Conversely, marginal fixed-horizon stimulus/outcome counts integrate generated histories, but can reward mainly teacher-induced mixture changes. Require frozen autonomous forecasts and separate reporting of stimulus mix, omissions and difficulty-specific responses; do not condition forecasts on the realized future sequence.

Seven MT mice and eight source mice can contain substantial trial-level information, but do not provide thousands of independent training replications. Shared cohort, source-parameter and trainer uncertainty must enter the decision calibration. One cohort cannot empirically estimate general between-lab variation. Include explicit shared-shift sensitivities and leave-one-source-mouse influence, rather than relying on animal-only bootstrap or simulation SE. Use all eight source mice, including failures; use all seven targets without endpoint-based selection. Do not infer whole-window coverage from expanded byte sizes. A fixed common-difficulty performance target also needs actual support under every candidate policy; unserved standardized probes are model predictions, not observed validation.

**Pass to scientific-design work only if all are true:** (i) capped method-9 decoding/row stopping passes the synthetic adversarial cases; (ii) prefix schema and chronology support replay, with every deterministic non-boundary next-pair prediction matching and stochastic choices within the predeclared policy's support; any restart/boundary exception is declared before checking it, not fitted away; (iii) remaining plausible deployment variants are finite and carried forward, including the Methods/code late-phase alternatives; (iv) a target and strong-baseline comparison can be written that distinguishes learning changes from stimulus-mixture changes without using future histories, and states how unsupported probes, shared-cohort shifts, omissions and source uncertainty will be handled. A prefix match is a falsification check, not certification of unobserved future firmware.

**Stop/retire this public-data route as the next investment** if the reader/cap fails, complete early chronology is unavailable, replay needs an unconstrained per-trial hidden intervention, or the late-phase policy uncertainty cannot be bounded before futures. Also stop before target futures if a subsequently authorized source-only development/calibration step finds no material model separation, teacher uncertainty changes the decision, or the seven-mouse rule lacks useful calibrated discrimination. Do not relax those conditions, select a favorable endpoint, or turn a successful reconstruction into campaign approval. No such development step is launched or allocated here. Passing this revision would permit the PI to assess a concrete scientific protocol; it would not earn an old gate, prove power or authorize all target outcomes.

**Strongest remaining objection:** even perfect reconstruction may leave all credible models agreeing on the already-known easy-to-hard trajectory, with only fragile differences on seven mice. Unless the frozen forecast discriminates learner transitions beyond difficulty-aware growth under shared/environment and teacher uncertainty, the work would remain a narrow reconstruction of published teaching efficacy. The proposed small revision is worth at most its stated budget because it can decisively retire a data route while preserving the larger question.
