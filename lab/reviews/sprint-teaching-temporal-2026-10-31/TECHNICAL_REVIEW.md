This finite review supports temporal-objective counterexamples, while identifying duration exposure as a material readout confound and retaining positive evidence of partial order memory. It does not refute the source paper's conditional fixed-timing demonstration.

## Partial order memory, publication scope and generality

In plant 102, reverse-minus-forward scores change from +1.935336 mHz after forward tutoring to −1.632754 mHz after reversed tutoring. The reverse target still ranks only 4/64. Partial temporal specificity and broader wrong-policy preferences coexist; total order-memory absence is contradicted.

Exact tutor content is privileged in order diagnostics; duration searches fix learned shape/correct order. Tonic drive matches only mean HVC input. Zero HVC retains sensory ordering, episode boundaries and recurrent time constants. Inputs are normalized signed synthetic sensory currents, not physical audio amplitude. These deterministic, consumed cases establish neither noise robustness, new-plant generality nor biological necessity.

The cached primary publication, *Correctness is its own reward: bootstrapping error signals in self-guided reinforcement learning*, explicitly supplies premotor temporal support and syllable-indexed evaluation/policy states. Its saved scope excerpts distinguish that task from this temporal-action/whole-episode-readout extension. No global novelty, failed reproduction or general learning-rule impossibility is established.
