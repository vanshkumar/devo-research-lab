### Certificates and their scope

For a common distribution over ranks, the union of conditional positive-rank breakpoints is a finite partition. In the observed zero-missing case, the rank-average particle values `q̄_j` are nondecreasing and the energy loss is

```text
R(w) = sum_j a_j w_j − sum_j (q̄_(j+1) − q̄_j) C_j(1 − C_j)
a_j = mean_i |q_ij − y_i|,  C_j = sum_(k≤j) w_k
```

Weighted isotonic regression gives the minimizing cumulative masses, with the fixed-missing extension using positive-mass-weighted target distances and positive-mass-squared spacings. A simplex tangent certificate bounds the optimal loss below the feasible loss by at most the Frank–Wolfe gap. An arbitrarily small identity mix retains every original atom. For the value map, the CRPS between consecutive observed target values is an integer count-error minimization over pooled original particle cutpoints. For an ordered pair of cutpoints, the larger cutpoint's loss relative to the smaller is nonincreasing as the target indicators turn on. The smallest minimizing cutpoint therefore progresses monotonically and can be attained by a single value map. The independently scored mapped atoms reproduce its optimum.
