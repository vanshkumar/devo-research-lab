## What the source supports

The actual reproduced exact100 equal-lab MAEs are 5.644 pp for the unchanged recurrent rule, 6.379 for the .9 session rule, and 6.276 for donor growth. Growth has the best individual equal-side MAE of those three. The session rule has the best pooled error, so dropping it on the strength of its lab average would discard a plausible fitted alternative. The historical reward-baseline REINFORCE is a credible history-responsive foil on the 40 matched contexts. Its original marginal CRPS .08289 is near growth .08097, while its high-contrast error and interval width are worse. Growth's arm null follows from its exposure-only coefficient path. It cannot represent the full ordinary-history alternative. The reference verifies archived reductions and transition calculations. Early recurrent states use fixed memory with marginal coefficient covariance, and late states come from generated standard training. Neither start ensemble is a new calibration, and the two recurrent laws are a sensitivity to fitted prescription rather than a posterior over plausible dynamics. IBL introduced 25%, 12.5% and 6.25% contrasts in stages and adapted gain and reward volume. It establishes those stimulus levels and the relevance of history, with separate GLM terms for rewarded and unrewarded previous choices. It supplies no support for 73% hard slots immediately after a fixed 1,000-opportunity prefix, prolonged pure difficulty blocks, or invariant engagement on those schedules. Lak et al. found difficulty-dependent reward effects on subsequent perceptual bias. A short recency model with stimulus difficulty, choice and outcome inputs is consequently a serious simple rival, even when cumulative stimulus counts match. The archive does not fit or calibrate that rival for these interventions. Myung and Pitt's design criterion already integrates over data and parameters and evaluates the fitted rival. Melinscak and Bach explicitly optimize cue–outcome schedules for learning-model discrimination. These precedents leave room for a useful design result about this assay and cohort cost, while choice of a maximizing stimulus alone adds little. ## What would change the investment

Randomization with a common probe identifies an immediate intention-to-treat effect on correct response, including omissions. Equal nominal opportunities and water per success leave reward exposure unequal. In early composition the unchanged rule forecasts about **18 fewer rewards** per H-rich animal over 400 slots, or **28 fewer** under the hard-trial omission stress. The 40 common tail slots align the recent stimulus distribution while rewards, choices and fatigue can still differ. A side-specific effect can reflect decision bias or engagement as well as a change in sensitivity. Conditioning on postassignment engagement or matching realized reward retrospectively changes the causal comparison. Record correct, incorrect and omission separately and generate future history under each model's own law from the same allowed prefix and schedule. Repeated zero-feedback choices lack a validated nonreactive interpretation. [Author screen](../../tasks/acquisition-probe-design-2026-11-04/SCREEN.json) · [Probe-reactivity calculation](../../tasks/acquisition-probe-reactivity-2026-11-01/RESULT.md)

At the generous 240-animal planning ceiling, the author's known-effect control detects 10 pp near chance with only .462 power, while 20 pp gives .930. A near-chance 95% interval for a simple balanced arm difference has about 12.7 pp half-width. A precise null would need a different information scale. Even this early cohort represents 336,000 nominal prefix and assigned opportunities plus 240 terminal responses, before attrition. Four sites support an analysis of the participating sites and supply limited evidence for transport to another. A negative result would leave the meaningful rival effects unresolved. A positive result could establish a schedule effect, but would have little leverage against a calibrated contrast- and reward-history alternative. [Known-effect and null controls](../../tasks/acquisition-probe-design-2026-11-04/SCREEN.json)

Reopen design development only for a concrete independent calibration object that links assigned curriculum and presented slots to actual reward amounts, omissions and common-probe responses. It would need to constrain the simple recency/reward alternative and engagement at the targeted acquisition stage, or support a separately validated richer measurement of the preprobe state. A future freeze would need a signed estimand, legitimate shared inputs, a prospectively specified simple generative rival, model and site sensitivity, and recovery of both a useful effect and a precise contrary at the actual cohort cost. A repeat of these simulations or a search over additional stimulus weights cannot supply that missing evidence.
