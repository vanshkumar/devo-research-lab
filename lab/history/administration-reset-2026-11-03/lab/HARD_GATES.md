# Gates for consequential research actions

Use LAB_WORKFLOW.md and the human's lab/INDEPENDENT_RESEARCH_SPRINT.md. Ordinary context access needs no research activation or worker registration. New chats, completed workers and paused tasks can read notes, reports, source and accessible history with normal tools, including .txt. These reads need no work slot or usage checkpoint. Dataset-specific protected observations remain separate.

Keep these conditions at the action they govern:

1. **Starting or continuing scientific execution:** PI checks actual usage, records the ordinary reservation and assigns a bounded Sol/max worker with ownership, permitted evidence and a stopping condition. Reconcile uncertain dispatches before retrying. PI coordinates and assesses; it does not execute scientific scripts, source investigations or quick diagnostics.
2. **Opening protected observations:** satisfy the existing dataset-specific access conditions and prospective freeze first. Keep Pan20, Flesch81/B2/B20 and later HABITS restrictions, exposure records and failed controls intact. A new chat or a read-only tool does not release data.
3. **Promoting a scientific claim:** require appropriate recovery/null/sensitivity controls and substantive independent review. Implementation checks are not independent signoff; reliable artifacts and scientific payoff are assessed separately.
4. **Spending or external actions:** apply actual human authorization and normal platform permissions. Reuse approval already given. A tool name or project destination alone does not revoke that authorization. Never spend/reset credits.

During this human-commissioned sprint, actual20%remaining allowance replaces daily2/2ceilings. Preserve counts, bounded work units, independent review and quiet unchanged-state behavior; restore saved baseline caps after completion. Candidate formation and pilots follow the attached prompt; a useful finite sprint may require multiple assignments and test/revise cycles.

The existing hook retains targeted PI/worker boundaries. It is a process aid, not OS isolation or proof of complete runtime coverage. Old canaries, contracts, permits, automatic receipts and exact shell formatting are not activation requirements. Do not grow another infrastructure project.
