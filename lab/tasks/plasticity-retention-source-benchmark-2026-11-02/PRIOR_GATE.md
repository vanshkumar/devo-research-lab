Primary: Dohare et al., Nature 632 (2024), DOI 10.1038/s41586-024-07711-7, [open text](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11338828/), CC BY 4.0. This work explicitly distinguishes plasticity loss from forgetting; its utility attends to current data. It already compares shrink/perturb and discusses full resetting and possible combinations with forgetting methods. The present question is a joint behavioral cost comparison, not a refutation of an unmade retention guarantee.

Renewal: shrink/perturb is an existing warm-start method (Ash–Adams 2020), also explicitly tested in the primary. The bounded search exposed its primary PDF and later renewal variants; no separate full-text Ash reconstruction was performed. Pinned Backprop implements SGD weight decay followed by independent Gaussian perturbations. Random renewal, shrink/perturb and replay are necessary controls; a total reset's new-task success cannot earn the present claim.

Joint/replay: [Wang–Chandra–Zhang, arXiv:2503.20018v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2503.20018v1), 25 March 2025, CC BY 4.0, studies the same task with replay as transformer context, not ordinary sampled rehearsal. It reports no comparison with previous anti-plasticity methods and tests current-task prediction; it does not establish the desired same-retention behavioral comparison. Its regression variant and its modified MNIST cannot silently replace the original task.

[FIRE, arXiv:2602.08040v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.08040v1), 8 February 2026, CC BY 4.0, explicitly studies the tradeoff and assumes past-data access. Its stability objective is distance between weights; it reports visual/language/RL comparisons. Weight proximity is not the same estimand as measured old-function risk. This is close prior, not proof that a joint tradeoff comparison is novel.

CVPR2024 Wang et al., *Improving Plasticity in Online Continual Learning via Collaborative Learning*, was exposed in a primary search summary describing acquisition accuracy and relative forgetting. BOTH HTML and PDF accesses returned 403. Its exact methods/results remain uncertified. Other incidental priors, including a thesis about less disruptive CBP alternatives, were not followed. No absence or exhaustive-priority claim is earned.
