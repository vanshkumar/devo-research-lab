## Research loop

1. Orient briefly using saved evidence and the agenda. The initial8–12candidate baseline and pilots are saved; do not regenerate a full portfolio after every study.
2. PI chooses among a few serious directions by significance, closest prior work, actual observations and a cheap falsifier. Delegate new source verification/feasibility. Do not make the human choose a menu.
3. Run substantive pilots or a deeper bounded study. Declare hypothesis, strongest alternative, comparison and interpretation before the main test. A download/import/literature summary alone is not an experiment. Inspect metadata/small samples before large acquisition.
4. Freeze consequential choices before new evaluation; preserve failures and timestamp amendments. Judge numerical reliability separately from scientific payoff.
5. Use fresh independent review and its highest-value contrary test. Revise, narrow, abandon or bank the result on evidence.
6. At a study endpoint, save an honest result/reproduction, then choose the next worthwhile direction above10%. Pursue useful investigate–test–revise cycles, not manufactured jobs, endless repairs or repeated portfolio restarts. Closing a weak branch does not close the lab.
7. At the reserve or human stop, checkpoint the overall run. For unavailable usage telemetry, defer starts and retain recovery. For a branch-specific blocker, switch direction; for a genuine lab-wide blocker, state the concrete missing condition and notify the human, retaining quiet recovery checks rather than inventing completion.

Verify an original model and its parameters with a relevant published/reference condition before relying on it. A new scientifically motivated perturbation or control test on that verified model need not already appear in the source paper: a fresh scientist may justify and freeze it before evaluation. Absence of a published version of the proposed new experiment alone is not a CLOSE condition. Material prior gaps, legitimate inputs/readouts, numerical references, finite resources and protected-data rules still apply; preserve existing closures and failures.

Leave a navigable README, runnable dependencies/configuration/seeds, actual outputs, source/data manifest and concise report. Separate literature, observations, inference and speculation. Check novelty before claiming it. A negative result can justify reallocating to another question.
