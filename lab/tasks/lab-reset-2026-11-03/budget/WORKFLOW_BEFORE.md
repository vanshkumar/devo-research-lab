## Scientific investment

Before main funding, the PI records a decision in existing BRIEF/STATE/agenda, carried into RESULT: the precise disputed material claim; closest prior; what an informed researcher would decide or test differently under plausible opposing outcomes; strongest simple or causal alternative; accessible required measurement or mathematical object; and why this test resolves the current obstacle. Accessibility includes legitimate inputs/readouts and existing permissions, not merely available files.

Check **counterfactual success before exhaustive verification**: if every planned test passes and payoff remains trivial, decline or redesign before main funding. Consequential replications, negatives, counterexamples and identifiability findings qualify. Lack of novelty or breadth alone is no veto; an arbitrary failed recipe is not a contribution.
