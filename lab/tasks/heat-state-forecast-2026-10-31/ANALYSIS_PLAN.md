# Development plan, 2026-10-31T17:50Z

Development verification earned 12 finite, single-pulse recordings with ~8min pre/post. No evaluation payload acquired. Development outcomes show near-complete pumping suppression in many worms and heterogeneous reversal change; a shared average response is therefore a serious competitor. This is a summary-informed retrospective archive comparison, not an unexposed biological experiment. The primary articles and mirror summaries were already read. Morrison/Young explicitly names evaluation UID 2023-01-05-01 in a perturbation demonstration; record that specific publication-summary exposure and report sensitivity without that animal. It has not been raw-outcome exposed in this lab. No silent repartition.

Endpoint: autonomous predictions of seven 60-second bin means at 30–450s after the pulse. Neural endpoint: mean squared error of prefix-standardized fluorescence across confidently identified neuron classes found in >=10/12 development animals; bilateral detections averaged. Each worm receives equal weight, missing classes are masked for every competitor identically. Behavioral endpoint: reverse fraction (velocity<0) and pumping rate, scored separately and as an equally weighted standardized MSE. Scales derive only from development pre-heat variability. Velocity is a supplementary endpoint. These are persistent response/distribution forecasts, not exact stochastic trajectories or fits with observed post-heat behavior.

All models receive the same prefix (-450 to -5s), explicit pulse time and neuron labels. Neural normalization uses that prefix only; upstream whole-record interpolation/bleach fitting and post-hoc labeling remain limitations. Do not use tau_vals, encoding labels or other response-derived annotations.

Competitors: zero/whole-prefix persistence, last60s persistence, a shared absolute post-heat curve, a shared response relative to own prefix mean, exponential local relaxation toward own prefix mean plus a source-shared residual response (tau in {5,30,120}s), and source-learned own-channel history/drift/observation features. The latter uses own prefix mean, last60s mean, first-versus-last120s difference, lag30s autocorrelation and coefficient of variation. All are autonomous. Neural prefix means are zero by construction, which is not future normalization.

Compact-state augmentation adds exactly four global features to the own-history model: pre-heat reverse fraction, pre-heat pumping rate, and two source-fixed principal components of identified-neuron activity averaged over the last60s. PCA is fitted on development prefixes within each training fold; missing classes are imputed using training means. These are measured-state predictors, not demonstrated dynamical mechanisms. Ridge alpha candidates {0.01,0.1,1,10,100} times training-animal count; standardization and every fit happen inside each leave-one-worm-out fold. Choose one alpha per modality by equal-worm development CV. Select the strongest simple family by the same CV, freeze both before evaluation. At most one later explicit development revision, no architecture tournament.

Check functional known-effect and no-added-effect controls with whole held synthetic animals. Check prefix projection by replacing every future response with a sentinel before parsing: predictions/features must be unchanged. Choose an investment threshold of >=10% MSE reduction in BOTH modalities against the strongest simple family, with positive animal-bootstrap95%lower paired improvement. This is a finite decision rule, not a universal biological boundary or a calibrated equivalence test. Pumping and reversing must also be shown separately so an average suppression cannot stand in for individualized behavior prediction.

If development offers no credible individual advantage, preserve that failure and assess whether a once-only held-animal test can still change the decision. Do not open evaluation solely to use the budget. Evaluation cannot select features, classes, endpoints, models, penalties or exclusions. Save predictions for every allowed prefix before interpreting any evaluation responses; a lexical reader may transfer/hash raw bytes and parse whitelisted timing/labels and strictly pre-heat numerical slices only. Its control and exact permissions must be saved before acquisition. Report per-animal errors, paired bootstrap intervals (seed20261031), stimulus-free/delayed-timing and endpoint/normalization sensitivities without fitting new evaluation models.
