1. **Active/passive matched-hand localization.** ['t Hart & Henriques 2016, PLOS ONE 11:e0163556](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0163556). Publisher HTML methods and results were inspected. Passive endpoints replay preceding active endpoints in shuffled order; active tasks necessarily precede their matched passive tasks. Generic localization biases are corrected using aligned-condition polynomial/linear fits, then applied to aligned and rotated responses. This does not calibrate a loading on stored motor state or a residual relative to the active learning-driving error in our factorial event conditions. Article is CC BY. The page flags a [2016 correction](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0168825); its direct fetch failed, so correction content was not certified. No numerical localization effect or precision estimate from this article is used to calibrate our generator.

2. **Continuous reports and time cost.** [Tsay, Parvin & Ivry 2020, J Neurophysiol 124:1122–1130](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7717163/), DOI 10.1152/jn.00242.2020. Initial full-text viewport and later indexed primary-text timing excerpt inspected. The report procedure adds 842 ms between reaches in this setup (3577 versus 2735 ms); intermittent reporting was adopted in experiment 2 in view of time-dependent decay. This supports pricing and matching elapsed time, not treating reports as instantaneous. Grid/rounding resolution was NOT certified: targeted lookup failed and must not be replaced by assuming landmark spacing equals response quantization. Publisher copyright notice; no text redistributed beyond brief paraphrase.

3. **Pointer/2AFC precision and context.** [Will & Stenner 2024, J Neurophysiol 131:1200–1212](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11389589/), DOI 10.1152/jn.00447.2023. Indexed primary full-text excerpts describe pointer and randomized-probe 2AFC methods and changes during early adaptation. Pointer bias summaries are 1.96±6.18 degrees in adaptation and .46±3.05 in washout (MEDIAN±IQR across reported subject summaries); these are NOT per-trial Gaussian SDs or report-to-learning-signal bias bounds. The 2AFC analysis uses PSE and a 75%-minus-25% JND. Context/strategy/attention affect precision. Thus a common small fixed noise constant is not source-certified. The article is CC BY 4.0 according to its primary text. No empirical record was read; no claim is made that the constructed criterion bias was observed.

4. **Stochastic estimation is established.** [Albert & Shadmehr 2018, J Neurophysiol 119:1367–1393](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5966728/), DOI 10.1152/jn.00197.2017. Indexed primary full-text excerpts inspect their state/motor-noise model, EM comparison and simulation recovery. Their error-clamp formulation specifies the error law, and recovery is conditional on the model. It does not certify an endogenous perceived-error report or biological-storage loading. The present run does not repeat their estimation/power claims as a new theorem and does not claim their published method is invalid. Article copyright retained; primary text only, no code/data acquisition.

5. **Closest perceptual-error account.** [Tsay, Kim, Haith & Ivry 2022, eLife 11:e76639](https://elifesciences.org/articles/76639), DOI 10.7554/eLife.76639. Publisher HTML and indexed primary excerpts inspected. PReMo models the difference between perceived hand position and intended goal and explicitly treats the current support as correlational with future tests needed. It already distinguishes perceived hand position from proprioceptive shift and discusses sensory expectations. This run claims no novelty for the generic state-space/perceived-error framework. Neither this source nor the inspected protocols validates our required error-signal/criterion or storage anchor. CC BY.
