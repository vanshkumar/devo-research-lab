## Planned prescriptions, not fitted

The comparison would contain exactly one source-fitted history/item baseline, a compact memory account, and that account plus intervening other-item practice. All share item shrinkage and a frozen source-estimated person-heterogeneity/state-update prescription. A compact logistic predictor could use item effect, source-calibrated online learner ability, log(1 + preceding correct/incorrect item attempts), and log(1 + elapsed time since the item). The augmented prescription adds log(1 + number of other-item attempts since the last item exposure). The history baseline omits elapsed time and intervening practice. These are predictive state descriptions; choosing logistic coordinates does not identify a universal decay law. Exact priors, penalties and numerical fitting would require source development, which was not entered after the readiness stop.

Before any target answer access, a completed study would need frozen equations/parameters, adequate source calibration, known-answer and known-null recovery under learner/item heterogeneity and adaptive scheduling, and synthetic past-only/current-answer/future-scramble checks. None of those scientific criteria is represented as passed here. There is no executable target-scoring or target-fitting path in this task.

## Outcome-blind support and stop

READINESS.json uses joint cells of elapsed days (boundaries 1 minute, 1 hour, 1 day, 7 days), preceding item exposures (1, 2, 3–4, >=5), and intervening other-item opportunities (0, 1–9, 10–99, >=100). The diagnostic calls a cell populated if the source-fit sample contains at least ten repeats. This is an outcome-blind description, not a post-hoc scientific significance gate.

The retained fit set has six independent people/222 repeats. The proposed source validation has two people/83 repeats; its answers were subsequently consumed in the explicitly labelled source field audit, so it is no longer an untouched future validation resource. Targets contain ten smart people/663 repeats and four spaced people/433 repeats. Only 214 smart repeats (32.28%) and 128 spaced repeats (29.56%) lie in populated source cells. These total person counts are upper bounds on support-restricted independent units; no larger sample is implied. Thousands of archive rows cannot substitute for independent learners.

The source semantics audit adds ten predeclared, metadata-excluded random-policy learners to the eight initially eligible source people. It decodes only 9,819 correctness values for field semantics. Counts usually rise by one for every previous/current correctness combination; excluded people nevertheless have counter discontinuities and left-truncated item starts. The audit does not justify removing the continuity rule, establish the reason for every gap, or demonstrate that all counter-complete histories are biologically complete. Learn/test meanings remain uncertified and unused.

The investigator stops because this bounded cohort cannot support a credible source-fitted, person-calibrated held-policy inference. No formal effect-size/power claim is made without a calibrated procedure. This is a design-specific investment judgment, not a universal information bound or evidence against transferable memory.
