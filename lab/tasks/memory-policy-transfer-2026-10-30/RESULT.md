# Conditional memory transfer: stopped before target scoring

2026-10-30. Scientific question unresolved. This block executed archive acquisition, metadata/cohort/support checks and a source-only field audit. It did not fit a learning model or score target answers. Status: completed feasibility stop; the proposed scientific prediction study is incomplete, with no validated memory result or novelty claim.

## Consequential finding and decision

The archive's large nominal size did not yield a credible complete-history cohort for this particular bounded design. Among the frozen German/B, stable-policy, post-gap installation cohort, the rules retain **six random-policy fit people, two proposed validation people, ten difficulty-policy people and four SELECT people**. Repeat opportunities are 222/83/663/433 respectively. Only **32.28% of difficulty repeats and 29.56% of SELECT repeats** fall in joint delay/repetition/intervening-practice cells with at least ten source-fit observations. Two source validation people cannot establish transferable person-level calibration and heterogeneity. Opening target responses would not resolve that source-design weakness.

Stop before model fitting and target correctness analysis. Preserve this as a failed readiness assessment of a specified cohort; do not reinterpret it as evidence that memory does not transfer, that interference is absent, or that all uses of this public archive are infeasible. No automatic fallback or successor is launched.

## Strongest objection

The scarcity is partly a consequence of **our** deterministic one-in-eight projection, fixed first-21-day window, complete-person counter criterion and shared-language/question-bank restriction. It is not a census of every possible complete learner in the full July-ended archive. Small counter gaps may admit a scientifically justified missing-history model or sensitivity analysis, and a different horizon may recover more repeat observations. The current work did not certify such a model. There may be considerable value in the wider archive; a large raw-row count is not by itself evidence that this particular source-calibrated test is ready.
