# Primary sources and scope

Gong et al.,2026, [reviewed preprint111771](https://elifesciences.org/reviewed-preprints/111771), supplies the sensory-cancellation evaluator, premotor temporal basis and syllable-state demonstration. Existing local source is sources/sensory_evaluator_feasibility_2026-10-28/upstream, pinned commit8309db5b1e2abb0d937fec7a1f9d434b06a9bd7c; exact imported file hashes are in FREEZE.json. Source dynamics and synaptic plasticity are unchanged. The independently tested batched Euler implementation changes compute layout, not equations. This study extends the published information setup; removing HVC does not refute the published supplied-timing result.

Gretton et al.,2012, [A Kernel Two-Sample Test, JMLR13:723–773](https://jmlr.csail.mit.edu/papers/volume13/gretton12a/gretton12a.pdf), is the inspected primary full-text precedent for distances between mean feature embeddings. Our fixed first/second-coordinate feature map is not a characteristic kernel, and the proposed variance-scaled quadratic distance has no universal distribution-identification guarantee. No frame-independent statistical test or new MMD theorem is claimed.

Elman,1990, [Finding Structure in Time](https://doi.org/10.1207/s15516709cog1402_1), primary abstract inspected, is the longstanding recurrent-context precedent. Ostendorf, Digalakis and Kimball,1996, [From HMMs to Segment Models: A Unified View of Stochastic Modeling for Speech Recognition](https://doi.org/10.1109/89.536930), author institutional abstract inspected at [TUC](https://sndbx.library.tuc.gr/view/55151?locale=en&show=full), is the duration/segment-state precedent. We do not claim the ordered sensory memory is a new model class. It is explicitly a compact, onset-registered sensory template.

The four focused primary-source requests were: two searches for Gretton/Elman; one search for the segment-model primary article; one full-text Gretton open. Four1MBreservations are charged in READS.json; no further request is allowed. Elman/Ostendorf were not read in full and no exhaustive novelty/priority search was performed. Earlier cached source/feasibility audits cover the Gong and grounded-vocal comparisons. No new birds, people or neural datasets were acquired.
