## Trial measurement and legal information

The release writer iterates the native trial log. Its NWB schema stores trial ID and start/stop times, starting and changed stimulus identity, change flag/frame/time, go and catch, all lick times and first response, reward time and volume, hit/miss/false alarm/correct reject, abort and auto-reward. The session object also reads separate lick, reward, stimulus and task-parameter streams. The extractor derives outcomes from controller events. Aborted trials retain lick information even though `response_time` is null and `go/catch/auto_rewarded` are cleared. Auto-rewarded trials are separated from contingent hits and misses. The emitted table therefore cannot recover the unexecuted planned go/catch class for an aborted attempt. Use actual event semantics rather than inferring hit from any first lick, treating abort as a miss or trusting the misleading public `hit` docstring. The schema supports a record of delivered events. Within-session order, completeness, raw/derived agreement, deployed stage settings and HDF5 ranges remain unverified on actual NWBs.
