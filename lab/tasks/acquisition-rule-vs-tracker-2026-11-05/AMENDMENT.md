# Development amendments, before further calculations

November5, after actual pilot inspection; no untouched validation is claimed.

The first5-model generated calculation completed64 animals x2,000 future opportunities x144 particles in133.8seconds. At2,000 opportunities the GRU beats the new growth+tracker on ratio CRPS and accuracy MAE, but the end-to-end comparison does not isolate the update law. The simple model uses a full-prefix static psychometric anchor and starts its score correction at zero; the GRU has processed that prefix sequentially. Add a strong prefix-filtered initialization check and a deliberately advantaged common-state simple growth/tracker challenge before crediting a learned-update advantage. A simple model inheriting the GRU's permitted prefix state is an ablation of the future updater, not a claim of cheap independent model training.

Growth must retain a source-estimated autonomous trend; a self-generated mean-zero score innovation is insufficient. Shared parameters, donor changes and step-size selection for the new challenges remain on the original fitting/selection roster. The evaluation64's outcomes remain consumed development. No frozen actor parameters, old recipe, source gate or historical validation result changes.

The initial generation uses the same per-animal starting seed for all laws, but the GRU's extra Fisher draws advance action/trainer random streams, so its paths are NOT common-random-number pairs with simple models. This affects Monte Carlo efficiency, not information access or proper marginal scores. generate_v1.py preserves the executed bytes matching GENERATION_FREEZE. Future comparisons will separate prefix, action and teacher RNG streams and use explicit common innovations. Both results remain visible.

At200 and sometimes500 opportunities, generated side counts can be zero. Their ratio is undefined. Existing score_counts reports a conditional-on-positive-count ratio distribution and its positive mass; do not interpret that short-horizon quantity as an unconditional proper accuracy score. Joint count/success energy and all-opportunity correct/omission scores remain proper without this issue. The ratio/covariance identity is evaluated only for cells with positive count support in every particle. The1,000/2,000-opportunity saved pilot cells have full positive support. The original reduction warning is preserved in REDUCTION.log; no threshold is repaired.

The prefix-only fixed correction-setting classifier did not solve deployment:964 contrast support and723 parameter failures, versus922/697 for the published default. Keep it as an unsuccessful input-law challenge, not a selected replacement or termination of the material question. The most useful later continuation is a source-grounded uncertainty model for changing correction settings/pool initialization, or a clearly conditional model-use result; do not pass actual future response-dependent inputs to any autonomous actor.
