# Closest original prior, checked November 5

[Liu, Geadah and Pillow, *Flexible inference for animal learning rules using neural networks*, arXiv2509.04661v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.04661v2), dated February5,2026, already compares neural update laws with REINFORCE, history regressors and tinyRNN. Its methods specify actual choices/rewards as update inputs, held-animal splits and temporal held-out likelihood; its IBL methods use12 preprocessed mice. Thus higher conditional likelihood, reward-history dependence, a neural-versus-history comparison and future held-out trials are established prior. The current64-mouse source benchmark adds a stronger causal adaptive tracker/growth comparator and explicit generated-input horizons; it does not establish priority for those ideas generally.

[Geadah and Pillow, *Inferring learning rules during de novo task learning*](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12621722/), preprint dated September29,2025, Appendix Figure9 already describes generated decisions and resulting emission likelihoods evaluated at real choices, alongside filtering and whole-trajectory likelihoods. Its31-mouse main analysis selects animals reaching criterion and fits individual probabilistic learning models; dynamic learning-rate decompositions also use inferred latent rates. Generated-decision evaluation itself is therefore prior. The checked text does not establish the current prefix-only, source-roster-matched, generated-adaptive-teacher comparison against a strong growth/score tracker. This is a bounded comparison of the closest primary texts, not exhaustive novelty proof or an exact reproduction of their published numerical results.
