# Priced next investigation — decision proposal, not funded

## Consequential question and actual basis

Can a source-fitted estimate of current behavior plus empirical acquisition growth substitute for a nonlinear future updater, and how much observed calibration does that cheaper forecast require at a fixed learning stage? This is a model-use and measurement-cost question, not identification of biological mechanisms or an effect of extra training.

The corrected pilot justifies this question. On64 consumed mice, the retained original growth recipe nearly matches the GRU over2,000 future opportunities: equal-site accuracy MAE13.51% versus13.23%, gain0.28pp with observed-site t sensitivity[-1.72,+2.28]pp. Its ratio CRPS contrast is.003475[-.009430,.016380]. The initial much larger apparent advantage was against a weakened new growth construction. A shared neural-prefix-state growth comparator also comes close (MAE14.31% versus13.60% under the common newly fitted omission law, point0.71pp, sensitivity[-1.93,+3.34]pp). A history/score hybrid can track nearly as well conditionally yet generate poorly even from the same initial policy. Thus state estimation, future update law and observation process must be separated.

These data do not justify an expensive search for a GRU win, a main consisting only of more particles, or a claim that the hybrid failure resolves the mission. They support preparing a calibration/state comparison only if its practical margins and within-source scope pass independent investment review. The64 are consumed and any new evaluation is postvalidation reanalysis, not restored confirmation.

## Identifiable comparisons

**Primary stage and horizon:** forecast origin1,000, horizon2,000; all64 have these exact opportunities inside the capped complete-session projection. Keep original40/later24, animals and observed sites visible. No3,000-opportunity target for all64 is proposed from a cache that stops before some boundary sessions.

**End-to-end forecast:** retain the unchanged original GRU and original growth recipe as mandatory anchors. Add one simple current-state estimator plus source-derived growth, selected only on the original optimizer/selection rosters. A defensible new estimator is a low-dimensional psychometric/engagement filter with source-fitted drift/process variation and a ridge/Laplace uncertainty calculation. Tune its current-state estimator and generative drift against held future forecasts on the original selection animals, not merely one-step likelihood. Current tests show why an online innovation alone is not an acquisition law. This is a new explicit forecast experiment, not repair of any closed neural prescription.

**Calibration amount at the SAME origin:** supply the new simple forecaster250,500 or1,000 behavioral observations sampled within the SAME1,000-opportunity prefix span. Use nested outcome-independent masks stratified by100-opportunity blocks, with multiple saved mask seeds. Release selected choice/feedback/omission records and their timestamps/contrasts; do not miscode unobserved responses as omissions. Do not provide probabilityLeft or reward traces that reveal masked responses. Public time/session/opportunity metadata can remain known. The missing-response state and uncertainty calculation must be explicit. Fit/tune the same masking procedure on source animals first. Masks are repeated computational views of the same animal, never additional independent units.

The GRU anchor retains its full legitimate1,000-record prefix; this intentionally asks whether a simpler forecaster can match a stronger-information reference with fewer recorded observations. At the1,000-record simple condition both have the same available behavioral history. It does NOT estimate an intrinsic calibration-dose response of the GRU, or treat an artificial GRU memory reset under missing history as a scientific control.

**Future-updater component:** independently give a simple growth extrapolator the exact GRU prefix policy and correlated prefix uncertainty, then compare its future law with the unchanged GRU. Keep the already tested history hybrid as a diagnostic rather than the main foil. Its donor increments must be fitted relative to those source prefix states and begin at zero at forecast onset. Both initial policies must agree on every cue. This grants the simple component neural initialization and its cost: it can locate incremental future-update value but cannot establish a cheap independent end-to-end forecaster. Do not combine this result with the preceding comparison into a purported fully crossed family/initialization experiment.

**Stage check:** use origins1,000 and2,000 with the SAME1,000-opportunity forecast horizon and the same250/500/1,000-record sampling procedure over the most recent1,000 prior opportunities. Compare calibration amounts WITHIN each origin. The later origin represents more actual prior task exposure; it must not be labeled simply “more calibration.” The full-history GRU at2,000 has access to more history than the simple forecaster's recent-window record budget; preserve that advantage and keep this check secondary. Primary origin1,000 remains the clean equal-information full-record comparison. Do not infer a causal effect of learning stage or extra practice from these observations.

## Inputs, observation laws and decisive endpoints

All future adaptive stimuli, responses, feedback, omissions and actor state changes are generated. Forecasts condition only on realized opportunity/session boundaries, so they concern indexed exposure, not calendar time, attendance or a deployable known schedule. Masked early outcomes and future responses must not reenter through trainer inputs.

Use the retained original donor omission law as the primary shared observation reference. A source-fitted history/filter omission alternative is a sensitivity; it improved conditional residuals yet generated approximately2% omissions against.45% observed, so cannot replace the stronger reference automatically. Preserve default-teacher violations. A bounded preparation may fit at most two scientifically interpretable uncertainty variants for repeat settings/pool history on original fitting animals, validate their pretrial input distributions on original selection animals and freeze them before64-animal evaluation. Never infer settings from each evaluation animal's future. An imperfect approximate environment is not a universal veto on empirical forecast utility, but no exact deployed-teacher or causal-policy claim follows. A robust model-use conclusion must survive the supported variants or state the narrower conditioning assumption.

Fix the practical primary target before execution: individual equal-side exact100 accuracy error together with proper joint(N,K) and marginal-accuracy scoring at origin1,000/horizon2,000. Keep count-only score, omissions, expected ratios and count pooling separate. Handle zero-count mass explicitly; no conditional-on-positive-count short-horizon ratio may be sold as an unconditional proper score. Use literal feedback success at zero contrast for auxiliary all-correct scores.

A proposed worthwhile decision band is2–5pp in individual accuracy error: an updater advantage of at least5pp with lower bound above2pp would warrant its future complexity; a calibrated simple forecast within2pp while excluding5pp would support substitution for this scoped use. Any such values need PI/reviewer acceptance based on use, not adoption because these data happen to pass. A wide or mixed result remains unresolved. The present9-site sensitivity half-width is approximately2pp; it excludes neither all2pp effects nor arbitrary new-site uncertainty. Additional particles do not create sites. A useful precise negative at a much smaller margin cannot be promised from this cohort.

Challenge must include scalar/reference and future-poison checks, shared initial-policy equality, source-state/filter recovery under independently generated known laws, a no-acquisition/state-noise control, calibrated observation-law trajectories, source fit/selection uncertainty and the strongest retained growth baseline. The current finite-law controls recover the known laws with .99975 power and a planted10pp count-level law with1.000, but those fixed-context tests are NOT biological power, fitted-neural recovery or calibrated model-misspecification intervals.
