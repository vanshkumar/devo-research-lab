## 1. Neural computation across persistent state changes

The interesting scientific object is a compact description that predicts how an individual responds to a perturbation and recovers. Showing a different neural embedding, changing encoding coefficients or a good reconstruction of already observed heat responses would be a weak endpoint. [Atanas/Kim2023](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2023.07.035) already establishes state-dependent brain-wide behavioral encoding. [Morrison/Young2025](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pcbi.1013818) already builds a mechanistic premotor model and reproduces increased reversals under a heat-inspired perturbation. That paper explicitly reports that its simulated response does not retain the persistent state after the perturbation ends.

A stronger prospective result would be: fit a fixed low-dimensional slow-state account on development animals, use a held animal's pre-heat recording plus known stimulus timing, and predict the measured persistent response and recovery of both behavior and identified neurons. Compare against persistence, each neuron's own history, baseline drift/calcium observation dynamics and one bounded alternative with post-stimulation changes. Supply the same allowed information to all accounts. A model receiving actual future behavioral/neural inputs is a different operational prediction.

The accessible processed subset has21baseline and19heat animals, identified neurons, behavior and approximately0.6-second calcium frames. Each heat recording has a single pulse. That can constrain the measured response distribution across animals and the usefulness of a specified shared description. It cannot reconstruct millisecond circuit interactions or independently replicate a perturbation many times in each individual. Animals, not frames or neurons, determine the independent replication count. Whole-record bleach fitting/interpolation also precludes a strictly prospective raw-preprocessing claim for these cached traces.

The unrestricted biological dichotomy is not identifiable: a system with changing parameters can be rewritten as a fixed system whose state includes those parameters; an unobserved modulator can make fixed dynamics appear to change. Thus a successful small-state forecast supports predictive sufficiency within the declared families. Failure supports the need for something beyond that state description, not uniquely synaptic plasticity or a changed computation. Define dimensions, available inputs and allowed flexibility before fitting.

The present baseline pilot tests a prerequisite, not this heat question. Adding own-neuron history removes the old approximately7%first-worm population-current advantage. It neither tests the19heat animals nor establishes absence of useful population structure. I would require distinguishable restricted predictions and a substantial held-animal forecast gain before a larger grant. A decoder improvement alone is an inadequate scientific payoff.

## 2. Memory descriptions across practice schedulers

The potential contribution is a transportable predictive memory state: a description inferred under random practice that remains useful when a different policy selects reviews. [Upadhyay2021](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41539-021-00105-8) already randomizes scheduling policies and supplies half-life/power-law methods. Another pooled forgetting curve or a repeat of the scheduler comparison is insufficient novelty.

Use the same learner/item prescription in two versions. Both use elapsed time, preceding focal successes/failures and the same source-inferred learner/item effects. One adds recorded intervening other-item practice. Fit in the random arm, freeze the full update rule, then score actual reviews under both difficulty ordering and SELECT. Online person-state updates may use preceding answers under that fixed rule; they are not new target-policy parameter fits. Evaluate calibration and paired prediction gain on histories supported by the source arm, using learners as units.

A successful result would show that the added recorded-history feature supplies information that survives a policy change. A well-calibrated null with adequate sensitivity could bound its useful gain for this particular representation/population. Failure of both prescriptions would instead expose inadequate source calibration or omitted structure; it would not establish a universal failure of memory laws.

The original complete-person design stopped with6fit/2proposed-validation/10difficulty/4SELECT learners. The independent review then predefined a focal-item candidate with75fit/15unconsumed-proposed-validation/62difficulty/46SELECT IDs. Its stricter no-detected-gap/shared-support view has36/10/29/23IDs and1,116/485/1,472/1,432repeats. No completed predictive comparison exists. The wider archive's millions of rows are not equivalent to millions of independent complete histories.

Completeness is the principal limitation: possible other-item gaps overlap87–92%of intervals in the full candidate. Recorded intervening practice is a lower bound. The narrower primary mask is retrospective and cannot detect invisible items or terminal omissions. Scheduler randomization does not independently randomize every delay, intervening exposure, engagement state or decision to return. A portable association cannot by itself identify causal interference, and conditional recall does not establish autonomous scheduling value.

I would fund one bounded source-calibration and recovery tranche first. It must demonstrate that the untouched source validation and actual selected schedules can support a meaningful contrast before target answers are released. The review proposes0.01nat/review as the meaningful gain boundary and recovery at a true0.02nat effect; those are proposed operational choices, not earned biological thresholds. Stop if calibration or discrimination fails, without another cohort search or target-driven repair. The existing archive is cached; no new experiment or download is needed.

## 7. Acquiring an internal teaching signal

The consequential question is what information an evaluator must acquire to teach imitation, especially when the learner must resolve correspondence in time. [Gong2026](https://elifesciences.org/reviewed-preprints/111771) already demonstrates sensory cancellation and simple motor learning, including irregular supplied premotor timing. [Sankar2026](https://elifesciences.org/reviewed-preprints/112141) already combines physical vocal synthesis and reinforcement learning. Combining their ingredients or making the sound more realistic is an inadequate novelty claim.

The desired computational result is complete acquisition on new target/plant families under a declared information budget: tutor exposure creates an evaluator that tolerates harmless onset shifts but guides correction of meaningful sequence/duration errors without being supplied the correct tutor-to-action alignment. Compare a supplied correspondence condition, one explicit mechanism that acquires correspondence, and appropriate absent/shuffled controls. A successful privileged true-error actor is essential to distinguish a bad teacher from an unreachable target or inadequate search.

A single exemplar also underdetermines which transformations should count as harmless. The project must declare the inductive biases and prior experience that supply those constraints. Its contribution would concern what target-specific evaluation is acquired within those biases, rather than evaluation with no prior structure. The acquired-correspondence mechanism must not hide a target-selected alignment or true-error calculation in its preprocessing.

Existing code makes this constructively testable without collecting birds. It also allows sharp architecture-specific failures: a learned evaluator prefers a wrong reachable sequence or duration, matched true-error learning succeeds, and a defined temporal mechanism rescues acquisition. Neither outcome alone tells us what juvenile birds implement. The inspected archives do not jointly measure tutor exposure, pupil motor acquisition, evaluator activity and independent timing interventions needed to identify that biological mechanism.
