# Brown prospective development freeze

Frozen after the actual Stage A audit and before any model fit or prospective score. Develop one fixed prescription on 40 Brown people. No Berkeley records or curves enter. The target is the joint counts of correct-feedback and omitted opportunities in a complete, fixed interval from 2 × load to 7 × load at loads 3 and 6. Each person receives equal load and equal eligible block weights within load. The independent unit is the person. Every model receives only that block's observed prefix and load and known block practice context. Within-fold training supplies a common environment law and calibration. The policy sees the current cue and its own past keys/feedback/omissions. It cannot query the hidden map or factual future. [Actual scope](STAGE_A_DECISION.md)

- Use five deterministic whole-person outer folds, with 24 fitting people, 8 one-step calibration people and 8 score people per fold. Share a regularized scheduled-omission logistic head including current legal history, practice, load and observed prefix tempo. Fit a strong count/recency softmax conditional on answering, with truthful action elimination and winner recall, rewarded and wrong action counts, history and lag interactions, practice, recent response state and key stickiness. Both dynamic prescriptions use those features and residual RL/WM/habit state. Three fixed fast-to-slow feedback-update/WM-decay components share fitted coefficients and source mixture weights. Replay the identical recipient prefix in all components. Personalization reweights by the recipient prefix choice likelihood; the matched pooled ablation uses the fitted population weights, preserving every component's recipient state, ordinary features, map law and common calibration. Ordinary and dynamic get the same one-step conditional key-temperature calibration opportunity; both dynamic arms share one calibration. This is a task-specific forecasting model inspired by the pinned RLWM, WMH and engagement alternatives. It differs from each original likelihood and from its factual suffix masks.
- Generate cue order by training-fold donor tapes confined to the measured precriterion window and relabeled to match observed prefix counts/recency. Reject joins with a triple cue run or any ninth cue. Retain any fallback and evaluate gross schedule calibration. Draw the hidden cue map from the training-fold, load-stratified multiset distribution with positive support, conditioned only on truthful prefix feedback. Use common random numbers for all actors. Give no fitted model a real future cue/key/RT/feedback/validity mask. Score targets separately. A poison control must preserve the forecast under changed held outcome fields and suffix order.
- Simulate 512 independent paths per block per model with paired random streams. Brown nominal, all would-be timeouts rescued using the most supported lawful key, and all rescued using the least supported key are timing contrasts. Keep two rescues at the unidentified upper rescue fraction, stress composition without an oracle and feed generated reward into future updates. These are corner sensitivities under a shared latent clock, with pacing/feedback transport and other key correlations unresolved. Report changes in generated law and score against the same Brown targets as a stress diagnostic only.
- Primary metric is the joint energy score on (correct fraction, omission fraction). Pair independent particle halves for the within-forecast term. Compare person-level score gains against both ordinary and pooled, reporting nominal simultaneous paired bands, Monte Carlo split variation, per-load count bias and coverage. Define practical scale from the ordinary forecast law shifted by one additional correct opportunity when feasible at fixed omission count, using the proper-score penalty for its own unshifted law. Compute predictive separation under each opposed forecast law and project attainable precision at 42, 85 and 91 people. Assess both observable Brown calibration and source-versus-recruitment uncertainty. No nominal score on this development fold earns a confirmation claim.
- Record actual training, calibration and simulation timing, physical and nominal storage/read cost, and price the design challenge, separately frozen eligible main, result challenge, one useful revision and closeout. Stop new science at the actual 40% remaining source checkpoint, even if the program would benefit from more paths.
