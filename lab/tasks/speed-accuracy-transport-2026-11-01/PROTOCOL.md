## Information and observations

Each held person supplies only block1 choices/RTs (96 nominal trials). Source people supply blocks1 AND2. Target condition, block number, four stimulus categories (high/low/very_low/nonword) and their opportunities are known. Individual lexical identities are ignored, never fitted or memorized. Primary outcomes are cell proportion correct and cell mean RT in seconds over interpretable responses in [.18,3] seconds, exactly the release's censor flag after checking its rule. This is a conditional valid-response forecast; it does not predict invalid keys, excluded RTs or omissions. Stimulus-category counts are known inputs, while target valid counts are used only at scoring. Weight the four categories by fixed nominal frequencies1/6,1/6,1/6,1/2; weight people equally. Correctness is stimulus-category=response, not the censor flag. The paper's [.3,2.5] cutoff differs and is a declared postvalidation sensitivity, not a silent reconstruction. Release metadata is not hardware-clock certification.

## Prescriptions fixed before evaluation

Process P: a seven-parameter constant-boundary Wiener model: a>0, starting fraction z in(0,1), nondecision time t0, four signed evidence rates (positive word, negative nonword). Fit joint choice/RT density conditional on [.18,3], with a source-baseline pooled center as a weak Gaussian MAP prior for individual baseline fits. One pooled-source fit, then one MAP fit/person. No across-trial drift/start/t0 variation: a deliberately compact process prescription, not the complete2008model. Transport the two distances from start to boundary by two global positive factors, plus a global nondecision-time shift learned ONLY from source block2. Preserve baseline drift. Process Q is the nested evidence-quality alternative with one additional global drift multiplier. Target-person block2 never estimates any process parameter.

Numerics: image expansion at decision-time/a²<.25, spectral expansion otherwise; numerical Gauss–Legendre normalization on valid interval. Bounds a[.25,4], z[.15,.85], t0[.03,min(baselineRT)-.005], drift magnitudes[.05,12]. Pooled initial values a1.4,z.5,t0min(.25,minRT-.03), rates[3,2,1.2,2.5]. Individual Gaussian prior standard deviations(loga,logitz,t0,logrates)=[.6,.8,.10,.9,.9,.9,.9]. Transport distance factors[.5,3], t0shift[-.10,.25], Q drift multiplier[.5,1.5]. Source mapping targets conditional likelihood. Two deterministic starting points for baseline/pool and mapping; retain lowest objective, report convergence and bounds. No evaluation-driven numerical/model repairs.

## Scoring and decision

For each person, accuracy loss=weighted squared differences between predicted probability and observed block2 cell accuracy; RT loss=weighted squared differences between predicted and observed block2 cell meanRT. Joint loss=half of accuracy loss/.05² plus RT loss/.05². Fixed .05accuracy/50ms scales express material forecast errors. Save predictions before extracting/scoring evaluation block2 outcomes. Bootstrap the EIGHT people jointly with seed20261101,10000samples; intervals are conditional empirical percentile intervals with no coverage guarantee and no source-estimation uncertainty guarantee.

P earns useful incremental support only if (i) joint loss falls >=10% versus source-selected strongest simple reference and its paired bootstrap95% gain lower bound>0; (ii) each of accuracy and RT point losses improves; (iii) overall weighted absolute accuracy error<=.05 and mean-RT error<=.05seconds. Report all references, P and Q, no winner swapping. A failure closes this fixed recipe; it is not absence of competence, evidence quality, shared drift or generally useful instruction prediction. Q-versus-P and individual errors are secondary, not causal identification. The outcome is a conditional first-transition benchmark rather than a novelty claim.
