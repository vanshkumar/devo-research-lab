# A six-response feedback assay did not discriminate the curriculum forecasts

**November 4, 2026 ET. Do not fund confirmation of the 40% versus 55% medium-contrast curriculum with this assay.** The six-response joint law offered little discrimination of the assigned-arm response once the models could agree on arm-pooled behavior. Against the source-calibrated online psychometric tracker, the candidate's minimum directional power was **.064 for the unchanged GRU and .054 for the session GRU** at 240 animals in the nominal screen. It did not reliably improve on the equally long ordinary or composition-matched reordered probes. Even revealing site and selecting the most favorable four distinct archived sites gave a power ceiling of **.121** for the candidate. This is a scoped failure for the tested curriculum, measurement, forecast laws and calibration scenario. The biological effects of the curricula remain unmeasured. The six-slot mean does improve *measurement precision* for a large curriculum effect. Under the nominal tracker and recurrent laws, modeled 95% half-widths at N=240 were 4.25–4.95 pp for the candidate and 4.30–4.96 pp for the ordinary assay, versus 11.70–12.18 pp for the first response. With fixed sites, normal sampling and a true null, about 119–161 independent learners would give 80% probability of a 95% interval inside ±10 pp for the six-slot candidate. Such a precise negative would exclude a large mean effect and leave the forecast differences unresolved. A four-site 240-animal cohort would require 576,000 baseline/assigned opportunities plus 1,440 assay slots, before shaping, losses or operational validation. The next investment would need an independently supported assigned curriculum producing a materially larger arm-specific path contrast after baseline calibration, with deployment measurements that constrain engagement and reward-history responses. ### Proposed memory for the PI

**2026-11-04 — Reactive acquisition assay**
- Observation: At opportunity 2,000 the capped 64-animal source supports both signs of 50% contrast, while only five animals per sign support 25% at the stated coverage threshold. The online score tracker reduced six-slot residual variance ratio to 1.02. The 40%/55% assigned-dose candidate had at most .064 minimum directional centered power against that tracker at 240 animals, despite near-perfect raw classification and valid later-effect recovery.
- Action: Close this curriculum and six-slot discrimination recipe at the tested scale. For any new assignment design, price the arm-specific joint law after baseline and within-animal dependence calibration, and require independent support for the chosen contrast range and deployment.
