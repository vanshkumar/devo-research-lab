# Concrete scientific comparison, conditional on feasibility

This is the design assessment required for the bounded revision. It launches no
fit, calibration or future access. The important question remains whether a
learning process inferred under one curriculum predicts acquisition under another.
HABITS is summary-informed retrospective prediction: published training results
and all fifteen criterion labels are exposed, and the inspected BW362 prefix is
consumed development/initialization evidence.

The candidate test uses all eight Random mice, including all three failures, for
source inference and leave-one-mouse-out development. It retains all seven MT
mice without criterion-based selection. A concrete *proposed*, not coverage-
certified or access-approved window is the first2000post-habituation opportunities
for individual initialization, forecasting the next10000opportunities. The choice
of horizon and all exclusion/censoring rules must be frozen before any further
target prefix or future access, after source-only coverage and sensitivity checks.
Never discard omissions/aborts or stop the scoring window when a mouse succeeds.
Missing whole-window coverage may retire this prescription; the known trained
labels cannot choose more favorable windows. No new target test can call the
already inspected first200BW362rows independent validation.

Four models must share observation rules, sensory stimulus encoding, side/history
inputs, omission/abort models, opportunity clock, phase transitions and the exact
online teacher interface:

1. A source-inferred reward/history-dependent latent transition process, with
   individual prefix conditioning but fixed source-estimated dynamics. Its animal
   state is separate from the teacher's online logistic estimator.
2. Sensory/difficulty-aware growth in exposure count, with matched source random
   effects, lapse/omission/history terms and prefix conditioning. Growth must
   predict conditional difficulty responses, not just pooled served accuracy.
3. A frozen-prefix animal response policy whose teacher continues to update and
   select. Any apparent growth from easier selection remains possible here.
4. A reward/order-insensitive transition control, learned from the same source
   information budget, that keeps the actual generated feedback interface intact.
   Remove feedback/order dependence from the animal transition, not by feeding
   fictitious shuffled rewards to the teacher or changing its sensory environment.

Each forecast starts from its frozen prefix-conditioned state and recursively
generates stimulus, response, omission, abort, feedback, teacher live/cache update,
protocol transition, and next stimulus for the complete fixed horizon. No realized
future stimuli, outcomes or elapsed acquisition history enter the forecast. Every
retained finite deployment is simulated; the predeclared decision must be stable
across the envelope or the route stops. Calendar-time motivation/restart nuisance
must be handled through frozen finite prescriptions, not fitted target futures.

The prespecified forecast object is a joint vector of nonoverlapping1000-
opportunity-bin counts by stimulus pair, response side, omission and abort, plus
phase occupancy and difficulty-specific response rates on cells with observed
support. Use an energy score for the whole-mouse multivariate count trajectory,
with a source-frozen scaling and a separately reported marginal score for stimulus
mix. A source-frozen weighted conditional-difficulty accuracy/omission component
must also improve over growth and frozen-policy controls; teacher-induced mixture
changes alone cannot pass. Report all four outcomes separately. A common teacher's
one-step factors cancel on an observed history, so conditional one-step likelihood
is diagnostic only, not the primary autonomous validation.

An unsupported standard probe is a model prediction, not an observed validation
target. Report the overlap cells and their coverage explicitly; require overlap
chosen from source-only simulated policies before targets. If MT serves no common
cells capable of distinguishing the models, decline the study instead of imputing
validation of unserved probes. Stimulus-mix and difficulty-conditional components
may disagree and must not be collapsed into an easy-trial claim of animal learning.

Before target futures, freeze a material advantage margin from source-only
prediction separation and meaningful acquisition differences. Then test the full
seven-mouse decision under known learner, known growth, frozen-policy and
reward-insensitive generators, null/tie cases, shared cohort shifts, source
parameter draws, restart/teacher variants and omission/motivation shifts. Treat
mice, not trials, as independent acquisition units. Include leave-one-source-mouse
influence and shared shifts that affect all seven targets together; an animal
bootstrap alone cannot estimate environment uncertainty from one cohort. Separate
Monte Carlo error from biological/source/shared-environment uncertainty. Require
controlled false promotion and useful detection of the declared material effects
across the finite nuisance family before spending target futures. If model
separation is weak, the decision is uninformative, or teacher uncertainty reverses
it, stop. No power, separation or calibration success is claimed in this revision.

A positive result would support operational prediction of raw acquisition under a
changed teacher in this small cohort, beyond difficulty-aware growth and a teacher-
only explanation. It would not identify a unique biological update rule, establish
between-lab transport, undo old gates, or establish publication priority. A null or
failed calibration would retire this particular public-data test while leaving the
larger cross-curriculum question unresolved.
