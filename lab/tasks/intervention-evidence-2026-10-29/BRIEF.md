# Discriminating evidence from deliberate training changes

## Question and decision

Can a source-inferred learning process predict acquisition after the curriculum changes? The old fitting route is parked. This task asks whether a verified public experiment provides new information that could distinguish autonomous learning predictions, rather than supply more observations of the same consumed source.

Different learning processes may predict different consequences of training order, feedback or opportunities. Exposure-only growth may explain the accessible endpoints just as well, or task and measurement uncertainty may prevent a useful comparison. A credible accessible intervention can justify a separately designed test; finding no credible route within this search justifies stopping the search. Neither outcome resolves the central scientific question.
