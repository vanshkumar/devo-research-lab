A concrete HABITS test would infer dynamics only from the eight non-MT mice, initialize each MT mouse from its first 2,000 post-habituation opportunities, then forecast 10,000 more. Generate choices, omissions, feedback, trial selection and phase transitions recursively; compare whole-mouse joint stimulus/outcome trajectories against sensory-aware growth and frozen-prefix baselines. These proposed windows are **not verified coverage or approved access**. Before target futures, require capped decoding, deployed-trainer reconstruction, frozen prescriptions and known-effect/null calibration with seven target mice, shared-cohort variation and teacher uncertainty. Include a teacher-only control so easier trial selection cannot masquerade as learning. Stop if predictions are indistinguishable or calibration fails.

**Strongest surviving objection:** public task code and complete behavioral histories might reconstruct the missing teacher logs, but that has not been demonstrated. Unknown deployment settings, small target N and endpoint-informed selection could undermine a seemingly successful forecast. HABITS already demonstrates adaptive teaching; Bak et al. 2016 already infers learning rules and simulates training optimization. Novelty is unestablished.

**Reliability:** high for pinned metadata/code facts; final offline reconstruction passes 47 structural/hash/source checks. Two directory parsers and original Git/OSF hashes cross-check decisive facts; this is investigator checking, not independent scientific review. **Scientific payoff:** a plausible new controlled-trainer route, with no new biological result, demonstrated model separation or resolution of the central question.

**Exposure:** published results for all three candidates and HABITS trained/failed identities are exposed. No behavioral trial payload was requested or decoded; the only subject file read was a 620-byte input-parameter file. No fit or outcome scoring occurred. Pan20 and all historical protected rows/frozen artifacts were preserved. Any HABITS study would be summary-informed retrospective forecasting with withheld raw trajectories.
