## The simple alternative survives

For the population first-response endpoint already requested by the banked review, one terminal common probe per independent learner avoids earlier probe-induced state changes. Its post-response feedback need not be nonlearning because there is no later scored response in that cohort. Separate independently assigned cohorts stopped at earlier opportunities can estimate a population acquisition curve. This costs learners and repeated acquisition; it does not supply precise individual trajectories or identify latent competence.

Six endpoints and two teachers require 4,800 independent learners/first-probe responses, plus all acquisition opportunities up to their assigned endpoints. Heterogeneity does not justify treating multiple learning-inducing responses from one learner as independent replicas of its preprobe state. Repeated full-feedback probes are representable by the RNN as an intervention on training. Adding them buys extra opportunities; substituting them removes other training opportunities. Comparing either protocol with the original acquisition path requires matched opportunity counts, feedback, session flags and protocol timing.
