# Side and contrast magnitude: bounded official-source finding

The independent evidence for **deposited side** is extractor code, not reward agreement. At official ibllib commit [`e7ed5339`](https://github.com/int-brain-lab/ibllib/blob/e7ed533947712685fd6f36b6de95390f510eb0a2/ibllib/io/extractors/training_trials.py#L44-L68), `ContrastLR` defines the named ALF fields. It uses `sign(t['position']) < 0` for `contrastLeft` and `> 0` for `contrastRight`, sets the opposite field to NaN, and copies `t['contrast']` (new format) or `t['contrast']['value']` (nested legacy format) **without a sign transformation or absolute value**. The docstring mentions signed contrast; the actual branch uses independent position. The official trial-loading documentation separately confirms the two field names in its schema example; it does not certify polarity or a normalization rule. The extractor pin is the most recent commit touching this file before the pinned affected object's December20,2023 09:08:38UTC last-modified date; it is dated November3,2023. That identifies a relevant official implementation, not the exact historical build that produced the S3 object.

The exact registered [iblrig training3.7.4 tag](https://github.com/int-brain-lab/iblrig/tree/67f80a964dd5c05751e415ef9d08b52bf03cfb46/tasks/_iblrig_tasks_trainingChoiceWorld) independently defines physical positions `[-35,35]` and nonnegative contrast levels `[1,.5,.25,.125,.0625,0]` ([settings lines31/56](https://github.com/int-brain-lab/iblrig/blob/67f80a964dd5c05751e415ef9d08b52bf03cfb46/tasks/_iblrig_tasks_trainingChoiceWorld/task_settings.py#L31-L56)). The trial generator combines the contrast level with `sign(position)` only for its signed summary ([trial_params lines259–273](https://github.com/int-brain-lab/iblrig/blob/67f80a964dd5c05751e415ef9d08b52bf03cfb46/tasks/_iblrig_tasks_trainingChoiceWorld/trial_params.py#L259-L273)). Reward/error event selection depends on stimulus position and its inverse, with no-go/error/reward paths explicitly separated ([state machine](https://github.com/int-brain-lab/iblrig/blob/67f80a964dd5c05751e415ef9d08b52bf03cfb46/tasks/_iblrig_tasks_trainingChoiceWorld/_iblrig_tasks_trainingChoiceWorld.py#L129-L158)). `stim_phase` is also separately set. This supports separating position and nominal contrast strength. It does not document negative amplitudes as an ordinary contrast setting or prove what generated the anomalous values.

**Why feedback concordance is not independent validation.** The pinned [`Choice` extractor](https://github.com/int-brain-lab/ibllib/blob/e7ed533947712685fd6f36b6de95390f510eb0a2/ibllib/io/extractors/training_trials.py#L79-L106) uses `position`, raw `trial_correct` and no-go, not an independent remeasurement of wheel direction. [`FeedbackType`](https://github.com/int-brain-lab/ibllib/blob/e7ed533947712685fd6f36b6de95390f510eb0a2/ibllib/io/extractors/training_trials.py#L15-L41) uses controller states; this code can distinguish omission/omitted feedback in its returned fields. Position/choice/feedback agreement is therefore expected in the old standard branch and does not validate what the animal saw. The observed inventory looked at **all82** negatives, including the omission and any row previously agreeing, not only the81original contradictions. It found no residual response/feedback discordance under physical-column labels in the qualified first3000rows of any animal and no other checked incompatibility. This is a property of the deposited ALF observables; separate task-stage mechanisms or exact deployed feedback equivalence are not established.

**Candidate measurement decision (not implemented).** For the intended operational full-strength side accuracy, a sole nonmissing side field supplies side, and the numeric contrast magnitude supplies strength. The narrow candidate is `abs(contrastRight)−abs(contrastLeft)` with inactive NaNs treated as zero; retain all choices, omissions, literal feedback, reward, boundaries, participants and time windows. This would reconcile a positional measurement of the **deposited** target; it is not a new conditional teacher or a claim that negative stimuli physically behaved like positive ones. There is no recoded cache, score or forecast in this diagnosis.

**Strongest contrary.** The exact3.7.4 source defaults would not generate negative contrasts. The deposited negative numbers might preserve a nonstandard/phase-inverted delivered stimulus, local code/settings, or a legacy raw/extraction problem. The public tag, closest pre-aggregate extractor and the two ALF fields cannot distinguish these, authenticate the actual version/pipeline or establish equivalence of perception/reward history under a real deployed teacher. An independent physical verification would need the exact session's screen-position/contrast log or certified extraction/protocol provenance. That is a new experimental object and is not in the released footprint. It is not needed to define the registered position/amplitude observable if the PI explicitly accepts that narrower interpretation; it is needed for a stronger claim about what the animal saw or why a negative was recorded.
