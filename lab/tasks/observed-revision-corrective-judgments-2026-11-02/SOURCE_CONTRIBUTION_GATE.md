## Why a new fit is not funded here

Afrin and Litman2018 already formulate exactly the basic original-versus-revised quality target, collect seven votes per modification and train a predictor using edit/length, error, specificity and linguistic features. Their NotBetter class merges harm and no significant improvement; disagreement, possible order bias and length reliance are disclosed. Liu et al.2023 additionally study argument-context-dependent revision quality. Neither paper is independently reproduced here. The2023code repository's entire`data/college`subtree contains only`.gitkeep`, so it does not supply the missing original-learner join in the inspected object.

Thus obtaining independent quality labels, showing one equal-length improvement, beating a majority predictor, or adding context would not by itself earn a material contribution beyond that work. This pass has not identified a new, discriminating, adequately supported held-learner correction decision against current-product quality and ordinary calibration. The absent identifiers in this CSV also prevent asserting a valid learner/assignment/rater-separated evaluation from this object alone. That is a reason to close THIS allocation, not proof no original linkage exists elsewhere, that prior models fully solve the field, or that independent judgments cannot be useful.
