# Established training criterion: real object, no funded first-passage main

The published IBL **trained1a** object is measurable from the available contrast, choice, feedback and session/date fields. Existing choice generators could produce its inputs under an explicitly supplied future opportunity schedule. It is not recoverable from the old pooled count archives, and this qualification has **not** established a decisive first-passage comparison. Do not launch one as a cheap replacement for the fixed-window test. This is not a finding that acquisition first passage is impossible.

The official [IBL criterion source](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/int-brain-lab/ibllib/master/brainbox/behavior/training.py), pinned as `IBL_TRAINING_CRITERIA.py` SHA256 `ee24aa2406dcd10a401508e59152b70971e4ae6c9990fbb0ea3eda0d4dacb101`, requires three consecutive training datasets, more than200trials and more than80%easy-contrast success in each, zero-contrast observations, and a pooled psychometric fit with absolute bias<16, threshold<19 and both lapses<.2. `compute_training_info`/`criterion_1a`, lines477/847, define the readout. Easy means absolute contrast>=50%; omissions stay in denominators. I use complete calendar training days, pooling same-date sessions, and exclude a partially capped day. This is a reproducible computational criterion, not a recovery of historical operator labels. The source explicitly retains suboptimal fitting starts/constraints for consistency. Its official five-fit [psychofit dependency](https://github.com/cortex-lab/psychofit) is pinned separately; fixed hash seeds preserve the stochastic fitting prescription.

**Trained1b is not supported by the current forecast models:** it additionally requires median zero-contrast response time<2seconds. Recorded response-time columns do not supply a response-time generation law. The current actors generate choices, omissions and feedback, not those times. No weakened1b or self-invented accuracy criterion substitutes for it.

`CRITERION_COUNT_COUNTEREXAMPLE.json` is an exact readout counterexample using the official criterion and fit: two three-session histories have identical per-contrast pooled counts, primary side-specificN/K and pooled psychometric parameters (bias approximately0, threshold14.05, lapses.11667). Their per-session easy accuracies are [.95,.95,.75] versus [.89,.88,.88]. The first fails1a; the second passes. Thus the old MAIN_COUNTS/SOURCE_COUNTS ensembles cannot provide first-passage forecasts. New joint session/contrast/choice/feedback trajectories or their sufficient rolling histograms are indispensable. The example is not a biological power plant or a new criterion.

I evaluated the official readout only on the already consumed original74 source histories within their authorized6,000-row selection. No proposed evaluation response was opened. `CRITERION_SOURCE_FIT.json` contains actual source outputs, not a metadata receipt:8 observed indexed-window first passages by3,000;10 by4,000;17 by6,000. At6,000,42source animals are known not to have hit through that budget and15remain censored. Finite-cohort event-fraction identification is therefore [17/74,32/74], not a population confidence interval. Among the49with selected rows through6,000, only7have hit; **10of the17known hits occur outside that complete-follow-up subset**. Selecting long continuous follow-up can discard known earlier attainments. None of these counts is intrinsic biological rarity or a calibrated neural/growth forecast. The initial censor summary error, exact correction and unchanged positive windows are preserved in INITIAL/EXECUTED artifacts and LOCAL_FAILURES; original fit costs remain charged. Sixty executed psychometric windows cost1.693CPU seconds, maximum.03634seconds each, plus preparation. Seven actual-criterion boundary controls passed their expected results.

There are further real origin qualifications. `CRITERION_SOURCE_CHRONOLOGY.json` finds14original source animals with earlier public training metadata,8with earlier active other-task metadata, and4with other active protocols inside the saved selected range. These metadata do not authorize their response payloads or rebase the old6,000 fitting window. The source calculation is a first hit in the **saved indexed window**, not certified first biological attainment. Preserve this scope and the existing source charges; do not invalidate the completed conditional fixed-window comparison.

The new aggregate metadata is already **outcome-related auxiliary exposure** for a milestone question. `AGGREGATE_CHRONOLOGY` inspected future protocols/session availability. Its fixed-window eligibility excludes NYU-48 after2,705training opportunities and ZM_1084 after1,056 because an observed other task intervenes. A success-driven task transition could cause exactly such exclusions; their actual1a labels remain unopened. Among the116fixed-window aggregate candidates, continuous standard-training availability falls to107at6,000,74at10,000 and28at20,000. Nine disappear before6,000 through another observed task. A future-protocol/continuous-follow-up filter is not blind to learning merely because no choice values were queried.

Consequently the192fixed-window roster cannot simply be renamed an untouched milestone risk set. A milestone analysis would have to retain the original132aggregate dispositions, including early switches, resolve left truncation/unknown session mappings, establish whether the criterion had already been met at the legal prefix, and distinguish attainment, task transition, observation loss and administrative censoring. Transition to biased/ephys training is not itself a measured1a label. Independent censoring/IPCW cannot be assumed from these records. A forecast of attainment under *continued* standard training after an observed transition is counterfactual and lacks the corresponding measurements; the current generators also have no protocol-switch/attendance law.

An explicitly **conditional** forecast of hitting1a before a supplied actual opportunity endpoint remains legitimate in principle. It does not universally require a new attendance or stopping model. But the endpoint/protocol history is privileged, potentially attainment-related information. A serious ordinary comparator must receive and use it; source calibration must cover the same conditional risk set. The current pooled-count evidence supplies neither those joint first-passage predictions nor their paired biological/source variance. Reusing its MAE or mixed-score SD as milestone power would be false. The old2pp/10%margins remain unchanged; the criterion counterexample cannot pass or rescue them.

I therefore recommend **no first-passage main from this tranche**. The exact missing evidence is a defensible prefix-defined risk set with explicit transition/censoring semantics and a source-only joint-trajectory comparison against both the retained/recent growth and an endpoint-aware ordinary reference. That comparison must establish separation and variance before a full confirmation price/power can be certified. The measured psychometric cost prices a readout operation only: at192animals×3actors×2prefixes×1,024particles, even one necessary-pass fit per path is about1.18millionfits, roughly9CPU hours at the source mean, before rollout, multiple day-end checks, source refits or challenge. That is a scaling warning, not a funded envelope or an expected biological cost. No speculative first-passage full budget, surrogate criterion, evaluation outcome access or new main was invented. The separate fixed-window model-use proposal below remains reviewable.
