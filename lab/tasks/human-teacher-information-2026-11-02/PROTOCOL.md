November2, after the source-grounded calculation and before any new forecast or
score. This is a **CLOSE at the scientific design gate**, not a preregistered
empirical main test. No main-test freeze, revised endpoint or utility gate is
substituted for the original comparison.

The allocation asks whether the admitted source supports a prospective teacher
information contrast that can distinguish learning-rule utility from supplying
realized future inputs. Its early falsifier is the actual deployed source
prescription and the original permitted early observations. Inspect the cached
main task, its builder, its ten planned input workbooks and actual first200
calibration in the already-consumed39-person numeric cache. Do not infer a
teacher law from bias labels or assign a new distribution to participant IDs.

The completed exact calculation identifies each person's source from at most
two visible signed contrasts, without feedback or hidden zero-contrast sides.
The cached task's main handler reads one table sequentially at rows0:600; the
separate random practice handler does not randomize those future rows.

This supplies no unknown future realization at the original forecast origin.

**Decision: CLOSE.** A uniform table mixture after unique identification,
independent Bernoulli draws from bias labels, masking an already-identified
table, changing the forecast origin, or selecting a different endpoint would
introduce a new information restriction or task. None answers this allocation
under its frozen original calibration and source protocol. No forecast or
score is authorized by this gate decision. The original positive benchmark is
preserved for its legitimate fixed planned-input information set.

Source-support enumeration, cached-prefix agreement and constructed ambiguity
controls validate this finite source fact. They do not test human mechanisms,
uncertain-teacher utility, engagement, general information limits, empirical
power or a novel theorem. All people remain consumed/POSTVALIDATION. The close
is a research-allocation decision, not scientific-goal completion.
