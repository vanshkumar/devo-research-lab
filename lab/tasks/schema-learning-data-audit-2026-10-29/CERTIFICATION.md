The two new plain-code files exhaust the allowed plain parser/task/export inspection route. `analysis.py` is model analysis with an aggregate human-curve import; `humanUtils.py` contains a moving average, node-label dictionary and condition-name list. Neither is the sought participant export/task parser. The saved tree also lists notebooks and pickles, which were not acquired. This does not assert what those uninspected files contain.

The named missing evidence is a version-linked task/export transformation specifying independent-person keys and inclusion, presented-event/option/probe order (including attention replacements), and omission/completion handling. Without it, a header or a plausible numeric state sequence cannot establish the observation law for a falsifiable human forecast. The brief requires stopping when inclusion or chronology cannot be certified; the investigator therefore stopped before using the remaining request capacity.

Reliability of the commit/source-integrity finding is high. Scientific payoff is limited: one provenance uncertainty is removed, but the measurement obstacle remains. The computational full-versus-reliability-one contrast remains consequential and unresolved in humans; no evidence here favors either prescription. Existing qualitative human findings and all consumed synthetic diagnostics remain exposed. A later study would require a separate bounded, justified measurement route and protocol decision; none is launched here.

Strongest objection: capped headers might reveal useful fields, and source-only extraction from an uninspected notebook might eventually recover the parser. That possibility is preserved. It cannot substitute for source certification or authorize notebook/pickle acquisition, guessed field semantics, or a retrospective relaxation of this block's freeze.
