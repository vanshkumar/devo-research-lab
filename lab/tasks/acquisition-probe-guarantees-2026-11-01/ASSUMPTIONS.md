# Information boundary for the reference check

- Complete observed histories and feedback-bearing actions are supplied by construction. A history can index a response kernel without identifying biological state.
- The teacher uses only logged history, is identical in the true and candidate world, and is known exactly. Positivity is strict on all 64 logging paths.
- Changing only the last input leaves the preceding training history distribution unchanged. Response kernels at a given complete history/action are invariant to the policy substitution.
- The target is the first response at a fixed final probe. No response after probe-induced learning is scored. Earlier probe insertion would require a new fully specified intervention; it is not silently covered by the unchanged-history argument.
- Original response probabilities, learning increments and world differences are constructed rational numbers, not empirical estimates. No distribution-free inference from the lab's finite relative scores is performed.
- Population trajectory KL is directly computed because both constructed laws are known. A fitted model's loss advantage over growth, cell energy scores, calibration plots and unknown entropy do not supply this quantity.
- The bound is a standard sufficient condition and is not asserted sharp. No finite-sample confidence, power, source-estimation uncertainty or biological coverage is established.
- There is one reference calculation and zero main scientific tests. Internal enumeration is not independent adversarial review.

Original empirical records, protected cohorts, earlier author code and consumed forecast arrays were not read or executed. Earlier source/practice/deployment uncertainties are preserved, not assumed repaired.
