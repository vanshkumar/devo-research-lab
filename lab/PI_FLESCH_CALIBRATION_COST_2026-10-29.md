# PI calibration compute check

2026-10-29. This is a source-blind planning calculation during the reserved source-calibration block, not an additional experiment or a target release. It does not alter the frozen meaningful-effect boundary or authorize target responses.

A known-effect/null demonstration needs full-pipeline replicates, including source fitting and source-only prescription choice. If one complete replicate takes R seconds, a finite N-replicate control costs approximately N×R seconds, plus forecast Monte Carlo and sensitivity fitting. Measure R before launching a sweep. Parallel wall time can improve only within the existing four-process/eight-GB ceiling; do not treat parallelism as free work.

The attached JSON uses exact one-sided Clopper–Pearson limits at 95% confidence. With one separately assessed control, even the most favorable zero-false-preference record needs 59 null replicates for its upper bound to lie below 5%; all-success sensitivity needs 14 replicates for its lower bound to exceed 80%. Two simultaneous claims need at least 72 and 17 respectively. Sixteen simultaneous claims need at least 113 and 26; 48 need at least 134 and 31. These are best-case counts, not recommended sample sizes or promises of sufficient precision. Observed failures increase the need. These calculations do not require separate full-pipeline datasets for each score when the same generated cohort validly supports paired, prespecified comparisons.

Do not accidentally demand strict certification of a probability equal to its exact boundary with high success probability: a valid confidence rule has low chance to certify a generator sitting precisely at that boundary. First report estimates, uncertainty and the predeclared readiness decision. An interval spanning 5% with a 5.1% point estimate is inconclusive, not demonstrated inflation. Incomplete control simulations remain an unfinished gate. The scientific question must not be replaced by a claim that a numerically correct fitting pipeline is ready.

Keep the 16-case semantic/person-family envelope transparent. It is not 16 chances to find a favorable outcome. Some cases can share invented cohorts, random numbers and simulation kernels; prove any claimed structural identity rather than deleting costly cases. Report pilot full-pipeline runtime and a precise remaining workload before considering the one possible continuation. No automatic additional simulation or successor is approved here.

Predictive utility and mechanism discrimination remain separate. If the two learners have near-identical useful acquisition forecasts, this need not defeat a prespecified primary learner-versus-growth test; it does defeat a claim that the data distinguish those learning mechanisms. The eventual primary is chosen only using source evidence, with comparison multiplicity fixed before targets. The question is whether one fixed inferred prescription transports, not whether every model can be ranked.
