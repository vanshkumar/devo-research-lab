# Frozen initial protocol (2026-10-28; before trace outcomes)

## Data and stopping rules
Retrieve 6 discovery and 4 untouched validation worms from a pinned public repository commit, as listed in selection.json. Indices are spaced through the filename-sorted baseline set, not chosen by outcomes. Validation JSON may be downloaded but is not loaded until follow-up choices are frozen. No sample independence across frames will be assumed. Maximum initial data ~100 MB; optional Kato public traces ~60 MB if accessible. Public mirror is processed data, not acquisition-raw movies; state limitations explicitly.

## Pilot N1: what does neural predictability establish?
Question: does a population predictor add information beyond each channel's own slow calcium history under a chronological test? This addresses calibration of dynamics evidence, not a test of the full LOOPER algorithm.
Preprocessing: trace_array (time x neurons), train-only mean and variance; discard nonfinite/constant channels, report missingness. No extra smoothing. Split origins into first 60% train, middle 15% tuning, last 25% test; purge 30 seconds on each boundary so inputs/targets do not overlap. Horizons nearest 0.6, 3, 12 and 30 sec with actual dt recorded. Baselines: training mean, persistence, per-neuron direct AR(1), ridge using current population activity and 5-dimensional PCA ridge. Ridge alpha chosen only on tuning segment from {0.01,0.1,1,10,100,1000} times n_train. Primary error: channel-averaged train-standardized MSE; primary added value 1 - population MSE / diagonal AR MSE. Report per animal, not frame-based p-values.
Positive controls: stationary noisy rotation (population prediction should beat diagonal AR) and independent AR processes (population model should not systematically win at long horizons). Run synthetic minimum before real outcomes. If positive control fails, repair or mark target inconclusive. No cohort claims about moving vs immobilized without matched measurement conditions.
Interpretation: persistent high correlation is insufficient; short-horizon gain alone could reflect denoising. Robust positive gain over diagonal AR at longer horizon warrants follow-up; failure is evidence about these models/data, not absence of nonlinear biological dynamics.

## Pilot N2: coordinated dimensionality versus smooth finite records
Question: is PCA concentration beyond univariate power spectra, and does it depend on transformations? Use participation ratio and top-3 explained-variance fraction on per-neuron standardized traces. Compare 39 independent per-neuron Fourier-phase surrogates (preserve mean and power spectrum exactly) and 39 independent circular shifts (preserve marginal distribution and circular autocorrelation exactly). Frame shuffling as invariant negative control must leave PCA spectrum unchanged. A noisy shared rotation must produce lower dimension than independent surrogates; independent AR should generally lie within null variability.
Interpretation: excess concentration rejects independent-channel spectral null in this recording only; it does not identify recurrent loops, biological mechanism, or eliminate shared input/motion artifacts. Null validity requires stationarity; surrogate effect disappearing after differencing suggests slow drift/spectral concentration deserves attention. Initial raw and first-difference preprocessing both reported; do not choose the prettier one.

## Promotion
After discovery, freeze one primary effect and code hash before validation. Deepen at most two directions across the campaign. Seek a temporal exclusion/sensitivity or independent implementation check. No novelty claim; prior literature must be checked. Preserve all outcomes and bugs in NOTEBOOK.md.
