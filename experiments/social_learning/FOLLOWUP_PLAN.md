# Exploratory follow-up, frozen before its computations

The pilot reproduced counts and exposed a provenance discrepancy, so promote this direction for a bounded audit, not as established new social biology. New analyses below are explicitly post-discovery. No untouched validation subjects exist here.

1. Compare archived-script and paper-defined regression targets using date random-intercept logistic likelihoods integrated with 40-point Gaussian quadrature. Check 80 points for convergence, verify the sigma→0 identity against ordinary logistic likelihood, and fit a synthetic known-parameter positive control. This is not an execution of R/lme4's Laplace approximation; do not call approximate Wald p-values exact published-regression reproduction.
2. Fit an interpretable behavioral family: one encountered demonstration produces log-odds g; a contradictory second demonstration subtracts r*g, with r from 0 (first-encounter gate) through 1 (equal evidence/cancellation) to 2 (recency reversal). A nuisance color preference is included. Profile the likelihood over r, rather than declare a cognitive mechanism. Three treatment means constrain this family, not every possible learning mechanism. Report relative fit, finite-sample uncertainty, and the assumptions needed to predict new sequences.
3. Quantify how many eligible trials distinguish a persistent first-encounter gate from symmetric accumulation after sequence A,B,B, using fitted g, exact binomial power and a known binomial enumeration check. This is a conditional design calculation, not a proposed unexecuted pilot or a claim of an optimal experiment. Include a range of g corresponding to weak learning because the current estimate is uncertain. Extra opposing trials, behavior before male courtship, and controlled eligibility would all change what can be identified.
4. Report the observed choice/eligibility contingency table and male-first-courtship patterns. These are exploratory diagnostics; courtship is post-exposure and may mediate genuine preference. All-mating sensitivity changes the estimand and cannot invalidate the preference-among-double-courtship-trials estimand by itself.

Use “all recorded trials,” not “all randomized trials.” Hypothetical missing-choice bounds are only completion bounds, not identified potential choices for flies that did not mate.
