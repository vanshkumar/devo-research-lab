   - Precise locations: PDF page 2, section 1 concluding paragraph, discusses convergence for convex criteria; page 3, section 3, equations (1)–(4) and following paragraphs, defines changing example distributions ending at the target; page 4, section 4 preceding figure 1, gives perceptron controls and evaluation; pages 4–5, section 5, describes separate shape datasets and switching schedule.
   - Relevance: motivates distinguishing optimization speed from solution quality, and equal-exposure/target-distribution controls. Our easy→hard block protocol does not reproduce their specific formal curriculum or experiments. It tests an order-based interpretation under a chosen shortcut shift.
   - Access: eight-page PDF text read; no source code or original experimental results rerun. No quotation copied. Its developmental motivation is not taken as empirical evidence about children.

   - Relevance: source for the identification issue. Our exact binary counterexample and noisy-pretest extension are constructions in this campaign, not a claim to reproduce a particular example in Holland.
   - Access: PDF text for relevant sections inspected; no claim about all assumptions sufficient in actual educational studies.
