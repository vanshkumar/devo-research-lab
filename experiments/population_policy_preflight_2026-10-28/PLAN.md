# Exploratory policy-swap preflight

This is a source-prefix simulation diagnostic, not a new empirical validation or a change to the failed individual prediction gate. No external prefixes or outcomes and no new learner fit are allowed.

Select the first four already-consumed source animals by SHA256(subject), among existing frozen RNN source forecasts with at least 3,000 future opportunities. Replay only their first 1,000 trials and footer-derived session boundaries. Use 64 paired particles, one 3,000-opportunity horizon, the frozen RNN and development growth bank. Reuse saved RNN initial calibration; the existing fixed growth/omission prefix-calibration recipe is allowed. Generate adaptive-standard and fixed nonzero external-style curricula from the same source-prefix initial state and identical opportunities.

Primary descriptive output: six-bin average, equal-side accuracy at the identical ±100% contrast, counting omissions as failures; also report its noise-free model probability probe and the campaign's original >=50% metric. The probability probe does not deliver extra training or feedback. The exposure baseline's probe prediction is policy invariant by construction; its observed high-contrast summary can change with stimulus mixture.

Report the policy contrast, RNN-minus-growth gap within each policy, Monte Carlo SE conditional on these four prefixes, and between-prefix variability. Compare the original-metric gap only descriptively to the root source-only optimistic N=20 threshold (~6.42 percentage points); do not call this external power. No outcome scoring, model tuning, or further selection follows this diagnostic.
