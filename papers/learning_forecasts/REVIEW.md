# Scientific review of the first forecasting manuscript

29 October 2026. Bounded review of the complete `manuscript.md` and `CLAIMS.md`, the matched-history diagnostic's declaration, report, result and relevant implementation, and the completed campaign evidence. No new outcomes, analyses or experiments were accessed or performed for this review. Historical campaign files were not edited.

## Verdict

**A credible, coherent forecasting benchmark manuscript is now present.** Its central result is supported: the frozen RNN recipe prospectively lost to empirical growth on the exact-100% population endpoint in 24 previously unused source animals. The matched postvalidation diagnostic makes the operational distinction between tracking observed experience and forecasting autonomous acquisition concrete on those same animals. This is suitable for scientific circulation as a scoped first draft. It is not yet a demonstrated broad biological learning-law result, an external transport result, or a submission-ready claim of methodological priority.

I found no central numerical or interpretive error that requires withdrawing the present narrative. The main remaining risk is significance, not an undisclosed positive result: one source-derived recipe, under incompletely reconstructed generative assumptions, loses to a baseline in a selected source cohort. The paper should earn its contribution through a reusable, fully specified forecasting benchmark and the prospective comparative result. Additional postvalidation model variants on consumed outcomes would not solve that issue.

## Highest-impact revisions

1. **Define “prospective” operationally once.** The public observations already existed; the meaningful prospective feature is the freezing and exposure order of this analysis. This is legitimate, but a reader should not have to infer it. Add to the first methods subsection: “Here, prospective denotes an analysis whose model, endpoint and comparison were frozen before the relevant public test histories were inspected; it does not denote prospective collection of new animal data.” Keep the existing distinction between the original RNN challenge and its subsequently frozen test. This is a wording clarification, not a challenge to the recorded freeze.

2. **Make the benchmark's decisive methods self-contained before submission.** The manuscript explains its information boundaries well, but the central comparison still requires readers to traverse campaign files to recover the implemented updater and empirical growth rule. A compact methods appendix should state the exact GLM observation and learned-update equations, what the GRU receives and carries between sessions, the prefix MAP/uncertainty procedure, and the growth neighbor features, distance, donor exclusions and propagation into forecast particles. Include the precise equal-side endpoint and population MAE formulas. These are existing methods to document, not new analyses to run. This is particularly important because omissions, reward coding and the generated trainer depart from the source analysis: a reproducible benchmark is the strongest plausible contribution, and “published architecture” alone does not specify the tested object.

3. **Retain the narrow contribution until its priority and audience are established.** Geadah/Pillow Appendix Figure 9 already evaluates generated decisions, and Liu/Geadah/Pillow already addresses held-out animal/future-choice prediction. The draft now acknowledges both correctly. Its defensible distinction is the frozen prefix-limited acquisition forecast with a generated adaptive trainer, a strong growth comparator and a new exposure-controlled source cohort. That is a plausible benchmark/forecast-evaluation paper; the present evidence does not establish a new learning mechanism or a general failure of neural learning rules. The Discussion's current bounded framing should survive any later title/abstract tightening. A useful final framing sentence is: “The result establishes a demanding forecasting test and a prospective baseline comparison for this implementation; the generality of the ranking across model families and fully reconstructed environments remains open.” Do not promote the conditional diagnostic or the future-only retention perturbation into a new primary success to strengthen the story.

### Exact missing method definitions

These items can be written from existing frozen implementation without another experiment:

- **Observation and updater:** `rnn_learning.py` defines signed contrast, its `tanh(5s)/tanh(5)` transform, the positive/negative contrast plus intercept GLM features, right-choice coding, and the nine-dimensional GRU input. Specify the additive three-coefficient update from a GRU32 and two-ReLU-layer MLP with a learned three-vector scaling factor. Current predictions precede updates; both GLM and GRU states stay unchanged on omissions. Primary session retention is one.
- **Prefix inference:** only the three initial coefficients are fitted, with zero initial recurrent memory, a response-likelihood objective plus `0.25 ||w0||²`, and bounds `[-8,12]`, `[-8,12]`, `[-6,6]`. The full prefix then produces the forecast state. `rnn_rollout.py` propagates rank-at-most-three Fisher uncertainty jointly into weights and memory; it caps endpoint weight variance and clips memory to its GRU range. State uncertainty is therefore neither a fitted free hidden state nor independent noise on weights and memory.
- **Growth:** `baselines.py` fits a ridge-one logistic GLM on the last 500 prefix opportunities, with omissions excluded from response fitting but estimated separately. Its four matching coordinates are three coefficients and omission log odds; fixed distance scales are `[1.5, 1.5, 1.5, 2]`. The nearest 15 development donors provide future 500-bin coefficient and omission-logit changes relative to their own last-prefix bin. Gaussian target coefficient uncertainty and beta omission uncertainty are combined with sampled donor trajectories; missing donor bins use nearby donors with that horizon available. The target animal is excluded from its donor set. This is an empirical trajectory-transfer comparator, not simply a linear regression on trial number.
- **Aggregation:** define each animal-bin target as one half of left and right exact-100% correct shares, each with all corresponding opportunities in its denominator. Define the population curve by equal-animal averaging and its MAE by averaging absolute observed-minus-forecast population errors over six bins. This places the absolute value after animal averaging and differs from individual MAE. Future opportunity boundaries are conditioned on in every method.

Source files are under `campaigns/learning_rule_transport_2026-10-28/code/`. These are transparent-methods requirements, not requests to alter the frozen recipe. Training objective/checkpoint selection and the uncertainty mapping used for original marginal scores should also be explicitly referenced in the appendix when presenting those scores.

## Critical checks passed

- **The two primary endpoints remain distinct.** Original 40-animal individual error at absolute contrast at least 0.5 is not subtracted from the new 24-animal population error at exactly ±100%. The second study is a new target motivated by the first, rather than a retroactive pass of its gate.
- **The matched diagnostic is correctly delimited.** Its population MAE is 0.628 pp versus 6.970 pp autonomous RNN and 2.278 pp growth on identical targets; it was selected after validation. It supplies actual future stimuli and preceding outcomes and uses a saved central state with analytic probabilities, whereas the autonomous output is a predictive mixture. The manuscript explicitly prevents an isolated-feedback causal interpretation and does not call this a new prospective forecast. Individual error is also reported, limiting reliance on cancellation in the cohort mean.
- **The session perturbation is treated as a substantive objection.** Future-only scaling of weights and recurrent memory by 0.8 reduces population error to 2.12 pp, but is not a coherent alternate prefix-and-future fit, fitted biological retention law or prospectively earned rescue. Its inclusion appropriately weakens an architecture-only interpretation of the original miss.
- **Gate failure is not overstated.** Correctly centered empirical-source-like forecasts pass only 16.34% of the calibrated constructions, and the observed bias intervals overlap the tolerance boundary. The prospective paired growth advantage, rather than the gate's binary decision, carries the empirical conclusion. The manuscript does not claim that true bias is proven greater than 5 pp.
- **External and causal claims remain closed.** The real fixed-curriculum cohort was not examined. Simulated policy differences are not observed intervention effects; realized future exposure and boundaries were supplied; laboratory and format selection limit transport claims. Exact software replay is not equated with biological identification.

## Recommended disposition

Keep this as one coherent benchmark manuscript and improve its standalone methods and contribution statement. It is a substantially better scientific artifact than another isolated pilot report. A broad biological-journal claim is not yet justified by the current evidence, but a carefully scoped empirical forecasting paper is a reasonable product to evaluate with external scientific readers. No further use of protected outcomes is needed to support the claims presently written.
