# Independent review of the older empirical/mechanistic portfolio

2026-10-28. Bounded strategic review by `portfolio_synthesis`. No new raw observations or protected validation were opened. Only this review file was written. Read: full root LEARNINGS.md and charter; authoritative reports and completion audits for quantitative HYPOMAP, song-target memory and Byrnes to-do; general campaign report and portfolio (no separate general completion audit exists in the files found); the two sensory evaluator preflight reports for the comparison below. This is evidence synthesis, not another substantive pilot.

## Judgment

**None of these four older campaigns supplies a stronger currently executable empirical follow-up than the prospective learning-model result already accumulated elsewhere.** Their most biologically important open questions require measurements that are absent, interventions that cannot be run here, or source metadata that ordinary refitting cannot recover. Preserve their completed results and stop fitting their same observations. A genuinely broader test of the sensory evaluator can be worth one bounded investment, but another local landscape or gain sweep cannot be the main scientific contribution.

The strongest open biological question in this subset is whether a selected tutor memory supplies the evaluative reference that causes later practice updates. Its importance survives this review. Its current *execution opportunity* does not: these reports establish neither a suitable public dataset with tutor identity/exposure, raw renditions and within-bird interventions nor validated selective access to the hypothesized evaluator. This is a reason to avoid claiming a biological answer from a synthetic critic benchmark, not a reason to abandon the question as unimportant.

## Comparison with the sensory-evaluator opportunity

The sensory preflight has a real advantage over these older empirical continuations: exact reachability, known independent plants, direct control of optimization and a runnable primary-source model permit a falsifiable test immediately. It also has a real limitation: we would be studying the optimization utility of a computational reward under declared input geometry, not the biological origin of a tutor reference.

The existing one-plant gain witness is insufficient. At offset 1, gain 1 -> .5 reduces circuit cost 2.0004 -> 1.8730 Hz while raising true feature loss 1.2740 -> 1.6370, but exact target gain 1 remains best on the grid. The true-error-optimal gain is .8408; some attenuation genuinely helps. There is no demonstrated actor trap, and gain scales signed post-normalization drive with premotor timing retained. The published source already examines input gain/error landscapes and demonstrates simplified reinforcement learning.

**The stronger test is whether frozen learned reward supports complete optimization through independently sampled, exactly reachable motor plants, and whether an identified property predicts which plants succeed.** At minimum it needs whole-plant holdouts, shape/gain control, paired starts/exploration/query budgets, an oracle that actually learns, untutored/shuffled controls, and true target loss as the primary endpoint. Both positive and negative answers can matter if a generalizing criterion is established. A negative result consisting only of a fixed-budget optimizer lag is weaker than actual improvement in reward while fidelity deteriorates; the latter must be distinguished from a stalled or underpowered optimizer.

Even systematic held-out failure would initially be a scoped computational finding about this circuit and encoder/plant family. A successful penalty using tutor amplitude/distance would spend extra target information and could obscure the intended question. The information budget must be declared before selecting a correction. A generic reward-hacking story, another bad direction, or a single favorable repair does not justify flagship status.

## Owner-facing recommendation

1. Bank the prospective autonomous-forecast finding as the strongest existing empirical account; do not reopen any protected mouse cohort to improve it. The sibling review owns its detailed limitations and consolidation merits.
2. For genuinely new execution, run only a small but complete matched optimization assay of the sensory evaluator. Continue only if oracle success and either meaningful circuit-driven learning or a persistent, falsifiable cross-plant failure condition justify it.
3. Do not reopen these four older campaigns in parallel. No source in their final reports currently changes the value of their frozen stopping decisions. Reopening would require genuinely new discriminating measurements, not a new chat, additional curve fits, or a relabeled synthetic model.

No broad new literature search was conducted, and no priority claim is made. The scientific judgments rely on primary-source audits already preserved within these campaigns. No nonduplicate project learning was found that needs appending to root LEARNINGS.md; the essential source, measurement and recovery limitations are already recorded there.
