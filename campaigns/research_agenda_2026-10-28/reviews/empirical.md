## Is consolidation itself worthwhile?

Yes, as a bounded scientific methods/benchmark account grounded in the prospective population test. It can show four empirically distinct levels: actual-history prediction; autonomous old-cohort average generation; genuinely unused-source population forecasting; and untested cross-policy extrapolation. The independently reconstructed failure of the third level is a real result, not just another synthetic stress test. The case is strengthened by a fair empirical growth baseline, known-answer success controls, original-byte reconstruction, independent generative replay, and a severe surviving session sensitivity.

Consolidation would not by itself add evidence. It should not be described as a new validation, a synaptic mechanism, a causal curriculum effect, a broad no-free-lunch theorem, or proof that autonomous models cannot work. It also should not combine the 40-animal and 24-animal numerical errors as if they used the same target: the first uses absolute contrast >=.5 with separate sides; the later test freezes exactly +/-100% with equal-side population scoring. Existing reports already contain the material needed for an honest bounded account; a new chat or polished manuscript cannot restore novelty or validation.

The strongest possible paper claim is therefore a carefully delimited prospective benchmark finding and its information-regime lesson. Broader publication potential depends on scientific positioning and community value, not another round of model widths or favorable postvalidation retention fits. The nearest primary work, as documented in the campaigns, already addresses held-animal conditional prediction (Liu/Geadah/Pillow) and separately fitted curricula (Geadah/Pillow). The additional factual contribution is this frozen prospective operational forecast and its failure against growth, not ordinary held-out prediction.

## Exposure and stopping facts that must persist

- All 74 original development animals and the 48 originally forecastable source animals' full indexed histories are consumed. The latter's later decoded rows are not fresh holdouts.
- The population campaign returned only the first 4000 rows of 24 primary animals and first 3000 of NYU-21. Later rows and SWC_007 values remain unexamined by that campaign. Those later rows would be partial-history follow-up, not a new independent cohort.
- All 20 strict external Pan-Vazquez prefixes and outcomes remain sealed. Neither original individual gate nor new population gate earned access. The prospective primary result does not require opening them.
- Target-event primary 40 and external Figure3 18 people are consumed; Figure4 and optional 210-person outcomes remain unexamined. All human-learning reserved responses are consumed.

The conservative population gate accepts correctly centered source-like forecasts only 16.34% of the time. Gate failure alone is weak evidence of invalidity; the separate paired baseline loss is the stronger result. The RNN bias intervals establish overprediction under their assumptions, but both the animal and lab 90% intervals overlap the -5 pp boundary. The .8 perturbation preserves a large simulated fixed-minus-adaptive contrast (18.42 pp), yet this is neither a coherent validated replacement nor an empirical policy effect.

## Choice for the owner

Retain the prospective result as an anchor while requiring a new computational direction to answer a different, consequential question. A sensory-evaluator study earns investment only if full matched-budget actor learning on independent reachable plants distinguishes useful teaching from evaluator-optimization failure, with an oracle that succeeds and controls that remove accidental target information. If it cannot meet that standard, stop this bounded agenda with the empirical synthesis rather than launch an indefinite repair queue.
