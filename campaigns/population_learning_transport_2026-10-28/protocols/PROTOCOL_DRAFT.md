## Endpoint and missingness

Primary bin endpoint is .5*(left correct share + right correct share), each
conditioned on stimulus contrast exactly100%, with omissions counted as failures.
Report numerators/denominators. Equal weight for animals within each bin, sides
within animal/bin, and the six bins. No response-only accuracy. Exact100% is
common to both policies; .5 source contrasts must not silently enter this target.
The noise-free±100% probability probe is a separate latent diagnostic.

If either observed side has zero100% trials, that animal/bin endpoint is missing
for every model. Do not impute zero or substitute a latent probe. The primary
gate needs at least80% of primary animals in every bin and at least15primary
animals overall; otherwise it is inconclusive. Population curve/bin-specific
denominators are explicit. Side-specific curves, right-choice share/all
opportunities, omissions, individual and population CRPS, trajectory spread and
partial-future results are secondary; they cannot rescue the primary gate.

## Gate under pre-exposure evaluation

Starting practical margins are scalar population mean bias within±5percentage
points (90%two-one-sided Student-t interval wholly inside), six-bin mean curve
absolute error≤5points, maximum bin error≤7.5points and each represented lab's
absolute scalar point bias≤10points. Small-lab values are safeguards, not
laboratory equivalence claims. Final interval construction, lab/stopping
controls, baseline rule and precision trigger must be settled using actual
metadata N and consumed-source controls before freeze.

The gate must pass known-answer controls for a transferable learner and reject
materially wrong mean predictions. High power for detecting a25point error does
not imply high power for establishing±5point accuracy. Failure to establish the
accuracy bound is distinct from evidence the true bias exceeds it.
