# Bounded primary-source challenge to novelty

Reviewed 2026-10-28, America/New_York. These checks establish the closest inspected prior; they are not an exhaustive literature search.

**Liu, Geadah & Pillow.** [Flexible inference for animal learning rules using neural networks](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.04661v2), version2,5February2026, §4/Fig4 and AppendixC/Table2. The paper already predicts unfamiliar animals and future held-out trials. Its §4 applies an inferred rule to an actual test animal's stimulus and choice sequence. The [published code](https://github.com/Helena-Yuhan-Liu/InferLearningANNGLM), pinned locally at `0ae19f7eec2836ab05487cc0342c7d1ac51a2727`, constructs shifted previous choices/rewards in `main.py` before computing current predictions. `models.py` supplies the recurrent GRU and additive coefficient updates. Those primary materials support the distinction between conditional history prediction and this campaign's generated post-prefix history. The RNN architecture and held-out prediction themselves are prior contributions. Direct primary text and the pinned raw `models.py` were verified through the web tool; local source bytes match the prior manifest.

**Geadah & Pillow.** [Inferring learning rules during de novo task learning](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12621722/),2025preprint, §2.1.1–2.4. The primary paper describes individual rule fits and latent dynamic learning rates under two training curricula. Its learning component uses the animal's actual decisions; the dynamic extension uses the posterior learning-rate path. [Author code](https://github.com/victorgeadah/LearningRules/blob/main/fit.py) exposes `training` and `no_curriculum` protocol options, individual subject fitting, and posterior/forward-pass evaluation. The locally pinned `ibl.py` at commit`0aa2edb7d05bef69ba544b143e885f956c8b9026` selects the Pan-Vazquez release for `no_curriculum` and matches Git blob`6de3b2b15e53caf8b396506589c2cdbb7405fc6e`. Both curricula have already been modeled; separately fitted acquisition curves are not a new transport result.

The bounded proposed addition is **a frozen source-trained rule forecasting new-animal population acquisition and then a different-policy cohort, with only a fixed prefix and metadata and with no real post-prefix history or fitted external learning-rate path**. A successful source mean alone would not complete that additional test. The present records do not justify a first-ever claim, a unique biological rule or a causal policy effect.
