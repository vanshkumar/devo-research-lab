## Selection and deviations

The initial S1 lead used adult performance-error studies; juvenile2025 source data proved more relevant and became the empirical target. Tutor-target causal interpretation required2018 intervention literature, so the initial counterexample was explicitly narrowed. I2's broad architecture idea became a more informative empirical comparison against an existing source model. C1's arbitrary-code confound idea was corrected before computation after reading the actual shared-code/update-gating proposal. Atlas TAC3 was exploratory after correcting a mouse-symbol query; co-detection and donor bounds were exploratory follow-ups. They are not preregistered discoveries or held-out confirmations.

Main follow-up allocation was based on useful constraints and accessible measurements, not the largest effect. Atlas and song received stronger computations, primary/contrary-source scrutiny and independent numerical audits. Imprinting retained known-effect recovery controls because its negative model comparison otherwise invites overinterpretation. No unresolved measurement gap was filled with a toy result and presented as empirical confirmation. This completes a bounded campaign rather than all items on the broad neuroscience agenda.
