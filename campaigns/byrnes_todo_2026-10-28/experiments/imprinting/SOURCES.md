1. **Freeland et al.** [Assessing preferences for adult versus juvenile features in young animals](https://link.springer.com/article/10.3758/s13420-024-00638-z), online16August2024, Learning&Behavior53(2025),145–156, DOI10.3758/s13420-024-00638-z. Inspected full relevant HTML: Methods “Predisposition experiments,” “Apparatus and stimuli,” “Procedure,” “Data analysis”; Results/Figures4–5; Discussion on hue/brightness/background confounds. Exact source URLs for four public supplementary tables are in data/imprinting/manifest.json; files1/2 sheet `in`, files3/4 CSV. Each row is a chick×minute bin; twenty bins per included chick. No acquisition-raw videos, original nonapproacher data or exact first-choice timestamps were analyzed. Publisher article HTML snapshot in sources/imprinting/freeland.html. Source reports first-experience behavior, not learned imprinting or a teaching-signal intervention.
