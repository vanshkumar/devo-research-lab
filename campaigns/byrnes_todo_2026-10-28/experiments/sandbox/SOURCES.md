# Primary sources inspected

- [Leike et al., AI Safety Gridworlds,2017](https://arxiv.org/abs/1711.09883), abstract/version record v2, inspected2026-10-28. Establishes prior use of hidden performance distinct from observed reward and tests involving supervisor absence and shifts. We did not reproduce their trained agents.
- [Langosco et al., Goal Misgeneralization in Deep Reinforcement Learning,2022](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v162/langosco22a.html), official PMLR abstract inspected2026-10-28. Prior empirical evidence that capabilities and pursued goals can generalize differently. Our finite calculation is not a replication of their experiments.
- Byrnes to-do post §3.1 motivates a discriminating sandbox. Numerical outcome features and policy family here are explicit invented assumptions, not annotations of real human welfare.
