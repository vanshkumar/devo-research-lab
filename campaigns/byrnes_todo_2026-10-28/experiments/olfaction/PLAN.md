# O1: public tracing evidence for bulb-to-hypothalamus signal

2026-10-28, before projection outcomes. Test: independently inspect Allen anterograde tracing summary measurements from experiments with main olfactory bulb injection. Hypothalamic positive fluorescence alone does not prove a monosynaptic, terminal or functional pathway. This is an empirical feasibility/calibration test for a proposed direct route, not a verdict on innate steering.

Acquire official small experiment metadata and structure ontology; select all qualifying main-bulb injections without looking at target signal, record Cre/injection coverage and target-set coverage. Compare source-appropriate piriform area as positive control, hypothalamus1097 and specific PVH38/SO390/ARH223 targets, and cerebellum512 as an off-route control. Primary metric projection_density outside injection site, bilateral hemisphere3, with normalized projection volume as sensitivity. No leaf+parent double counting. Query injection unionizes separately to diagnose off-target contamination; predefine ≥90% injection signal in MOB as a cleaner subset, with80% sensitivity. Reporter-level assay cannot resolve OR37 cell specificity.

Interpretation before outcomes: robust target signal exceeding off-route control with piriform recovery and clean injection supports further tracing-image examination, not direct synapses or behavior. No signal cannot exclude a sparse OR37/ghrelin pathway because injection sampling and resolution may miss it. Failed piriform control makes target inconclusive. If only contaminated injections show signal, injection confounding is an alternative. No frame/voxel pseudoreplication; report per experiment with absolute values, not an arbitrary binary significance cutoff.

Resource cap initial10MB metadata/unionizes; no whole3D imaging downloads. Legitimate public endpoints only. Primary Bader2012/Russo2018 papers inform interpretation and contrary evidence; no author contact is authorized. Any cohort filter/metric change after outcomes is exploratory and recorded.
