# Song-learning pilot — pre-outcome protocol, 2026-10-28

Question from Byrnes §2.1: what evidence constrains how a learned vocal target becomes a teaching signal? Nearest available substantive test: inspect juvenile developmental dopamine/song evidence, not infer an internal tutor template from recordings of song alone.

## Selection and prior knowledge

Literature search found Gadagkar2016 adult distortion responses, Duffy2022 natural adult fluctuations, and Kasdin/Duffy2025 juvenile development. Raw 2022 data are request-only; its public Figshare archive contains code/synthetic examples. The 2025 paper provides public source XLSX and was corrected in2026 for audio clipping. Primary captions report25 syllables; therefore this is exploratory reproduction/robustness from known published results, not a blind discovery or independent subjects. No author contact or inaccessible raw data retrieval.

## Test before opening source tables

Acquire source Fig3 (future song evolution), Fig4 (prediction-error/history comparison) and Extended8 (future-versus-past controls), each ≤15MB and total≤50MB. Save hashes/URLs. Inspect labels/units/IDs before selecting a computation. Prefer per-syllable paired future versus past values and leave-one-bird-out sensitivity if bird IDs are present. If source data are summary traces with no bird mapping, report descriptive reproduction only; never treat frames, renditions or syllables as independent birds.

Primary empirical target: whether dopamine-aligned song change exceeds its provided shuffled baseline and whether future-versus-past contrast survives the available appropriate-unit sensitivity. Rival: global song maturation, autocorrelated drift or a common upstream teaching signal could yield association without dopamine causation. Positive control: reproduce a known sign/summary in the source table; independently check an exact paired/count calculation where possible.

If raw per-rendition data are unavailable, add a separate controlled identifiability check: model a common latent target/error driving both dopamine and later song update, compare it with a dopamine-causal updater, and verify whether observational lag and future-alignment statistics distinguish the mechanisms. This is one pilot's adversarial test, not a new biological pilot. A perturbation/intervention prediction must differ, or the counterexample is not useful.

Interpretation: successful source reproduction supports stated association under its measurements; it cannot explain tutor-memory acquisition, grounding of target value, or causal dopaminergic necessity/sufficiency. Selected adult endpoint may be a proxy rather than actual tutor template. Failed source positive control makes target claim inconclusive until repaired. Updated source provenance must be recorded; do not reanalyze known-clipped audio or claim to reproduce a corrected raw pipeline from aggregate tables.

Stop after one substantive empirical test plus bounded controlled check. Save all attempted outcomes and a keep/revise/retire/blocked decision. Promoted follow-up requires root selection, not just a favorable p-value.
