## Promoted follow-up: inspected precedent and contrary evidence

- [Xi, Kusano & Gainer1999, PMID10499525](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10499525/), *Quantitative analysis…single magnocellular neurons…rat hypothalamus*. Primary abstract inspected through PubMed search extraction; full PubMed open returned a stripped page, so no complete paper/proof claim. Qualitative detections of both transcripts were common, while quantitative ratios preserved dominant peptide phenotypes. This defeats novelty and the inference that binary co-detection erases all cell-type distinctions. It is rat, not a matched human–mouse experiment.
- [Mezey & Kiss1991, PMID1915070](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/1915070/), DOI10.1210/endo-129-4-1814. Primary abstract inspected: direct in-situ/immunohistochemical/ultrastructural coexpression under lactation; distinguishes biological state and molecular measurement. No data reanalysis or matched species comparison.
- [Kiyama & Emson1990, PMID19215343](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19215343/), DOI10.1111/j.1365-2826.1990.tb00401.x. Primary abstract and publisher search-extracted first-page method inspected: simultaneous transcript detection in salt-loaded rat, mainly separate phenotypes but some double-labelled cells. Different assay/sampling/state precludes numeric comparison to our selected human cluster.
- [Sukhov et al.1993, PMC9883978](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9883978/), DOI10.1002/cne.903370210. Full relevant primary Results/Discussion text inspected through cached source extraction: spatial segregation of human signals, but the authors explicitly could not test same-cell coexpression. It is contrary context, not direct evidence of zero overlap. Later direct opens returned a browser check; not bypassed.
- [Caglayan, Liu & Konopka2022, PMID36240767](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36240767/), DOI10.1016/j.neuron.2022.09.010. Primary abstract plus introduction/search-extracted source text inspected: brain ambient RNA can change cell-type annotation. This is not a measured OXT/AVP artifact rate in HYPOMAP, so no quantitative contamination correction is imported.
- HYPOMAP Methods “Sequence alignment, cell calling and quality control” explicitly includes CellBender. That is a relevant existing safeguard, not proof residual false detections are absent. The pinned `stratified_wilcoxon_functions.R`284–293 calculates pct.1/pct.2 as three-decimal positive-expression fractions **before** optional downsampling at348–355. `supplementary_tables.R`172–188 calculates clusterN and two-decimal donor percentages from the same metadata grouping. Marker script/parameters were acquired and hash-verified; the unavailable exact final-table processing version remains a scope assumption.
