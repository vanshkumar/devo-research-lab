## Informative replacement decision

These are recommendations for the lead to instantiate and test **before** any independent outcome; they are not an already calibrated rule.

Keep the working primary endpoint: equal-side correct share on exact±100% contrasts, omissions as failures, six500-opportunity bins. Never mix the old≥50% endpoint into it. Require both observed sides in each scored animal/bin; define missingness and at least80%animal coverage in every bin before scoring. Given a four-contrast uniform trainer, a500-opportunity bin has about62 opportunities per signed100% contrast in expectation; no empirical coverage is asserted until an allowed input audit.

The primary utility contrast should be the paired difference in **population curve MAE**, computed after equal-weight animal averaging in each bin. Preserve the old external useful-improvement magnitude of5pp. A convincing utility result requires a point gain of at least5pp over growth and persistence and a paired95%interval lower gain above0. Use one selected generative law; report the other prespecified law and CRPS as checks, not a second chance at the primary decision. Report absolute mean-curve error, maximum bin error, scalar bias/intervals, omissions and individual error so a baseline defeat cannot conceal a poor absolute forecast.

Do not make the old combination of dual equivalence intervals, every-lab limits and leave-one-lab limits the new release rule. One external environment cannot support a new-lab equivalence claim, and an omnibus control with16%correct acceptance is poor evidence that a negative gate is informative. A useful absolute-error bound can remain a scientific success requirement if calibrated sensitivity supports it; otherwise distinguish “comparative utility” from “absolute calibration established” explicitly.

Resample animals **with all six bins jointly** and recompute population curves and their losses in every paired replicate. Do not treat six bins or512 simulation particles as independent biological samples. Separate conditional MonteCarlo error from across-animal uncertainty. Keep the one-environment scope visible; there is no external laboratory-cluster interval. At N20, the already observed source scalar residualSD10.55pp implies an approximate scalar95%halfwidth4.94pp, so mere failure of±5pp equivalence will often be inconclusive.

Before prefix release, run the exact selected uncertainty/decision procedure on finite controls at N20 using (a) centered empirical source residual vectors, preserving six-bin covariance; (b) a known generative learner under both trainers with fresh independent future streams; (c) growth/persistence truth and equal-forecast controls; and (d) modest heterogeneity/shared-offset constructions. Predeclare at least80%ability to choose the correct centered candidate when its predictions are separated by10pp from growth, at most5%false superiority under the baseline/equal controls, and adequate interval coverage under the stated centered construction. Pin MonteCarlo seeds/counts and report binomial uncertainty. These are design requirements, not guarantees under an arbitrary biological environment. If they fail, stop rather than loosening them after external data.

After lawful prefix-only forecasts, require the actual-cohort selected-law versus growth gap to be at least10pp on a prespecified scalar/curve definition and require sensitivity variants to preserve a discriminating contrast. The10pp value retains the old prospective separation requirement and supplies room for a5pp material utility decision. A source-prefix policy contrast does not establish the required gap for Pan prefix states. If those forecasts converge, keep scored futures sealed: this dataset would not distinguish the proposed answers at the intended scale.
