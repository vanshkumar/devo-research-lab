## The scientific question and what this block changes

Can a process inferred under standard adaptive training forecast acquisition under a different trainer after1000 observed opportunities, generating the next3000 choices, omissions and feedback, and materially outperform exposure growth with useful uncertainty? A positive independent prediction would support that practical use beyond the fitting environment. It would not identify a unique biological mechanism or a causal curriculum benefit.

The launch preserved this question after two earlier source-gate failures. The [full-index metadata check](../../sources/learning_transport_route_2026-10-29/REPORT.md) had certified no fresh ordinary-source acquisition cohort in that index. We therefore explicitly designed a possible single-environment utility test using **all exposed source observations as development evidence**. An external failure would retain ambiguity between ordinary generalization and transport failure. No fresh source confirmation was claimed or substituted.

This block did substantive fitting,256comparison forecasts,42,000known-answer decision evaluations, independent reconstruction and a synthetic transport control. Its scientific result is a concrete stop for this implementation and validation prescription. A better pooled source mean does not establish consistent laboratory performance, and the proposed external decision is insufficiently sensitive in its declared empirical-noise control. Neither finding is evidence that transferable biological learning processes are impossible.
