# Sources actually used

All study sources are public. Downloaded bytes are pinned locally; publication-tag caches are mutable. No accounts, paid services, author contact or private uploads were used. The original feasibility record is retained intact at `sources/longitudinal_learning_feasibility_2026-10-28/` from the repository root.

## Learning models and close prior

- Liu, Geadah & Pillow, [Flexible inference for animal learning rules using neural networks](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.04661v2), arXiv2509.04661v2,5February2026. Read primary text and exact [source repository](https://github.com/Helena-Yuhan-Liu/InferLearningANNGLM) at `0ae19f7eec2836ab05487cc0342c7d1ac51a2727`. Original files are under `artifacts/models/source/`, with SHA256/Git-blob verification. The DNNGLM additive recurrence, two ReLU layers, three-coefficient supported mode, contrast transform, previous-input shift and reward baseline are reconstructed. This is an architecture/recurrence reproduction on a new allocation, not an exact replication of the paper's12-animal numerical experiment. That paper conditions on preceding real decisions/rewards; our autonomous test supplies generated histories. Its pinned notebook drops omission rows; this study retains them and explicitly extends the observation process. See `protocols/MODEL.md` and `SOURCE_RECURRENCE_AUDIT.json`.
- Geadah & Pillow, [Inferring learning rules during de novo task learning](https://doi.org/10.1101/2025.09.29.679295), pinned [LearningRules code](https://github.com/victorgeadah/LearningRules) `0aa2edb7d05bef69ba544b143e885f956c8b9026`. Cached `geadah_ibl.py` and repository tree were reused for preprocessing/cohort scope. The feasibility primary review identified separately fitted dynamics across the curricula. Our test freezes a source rule before a potential external forecast. The lead's direct PMC browser retrieval returned a recaptcha page; it was not treated as paper evidence.
- Pan-Vazquez et al., [Pre-existing visual responses in a projection-defined dopamine population explain individual learning trajectories](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cub.2024.09.045), Current Biology2024; [author code](https://github.com/YSanchezAraujo/PRE_VIS_DA_CURR_BIO) `3a5506eaf47d2a0c085c5a5f90e4a2c651c03043`. Publication methods, author mapping and input-only checks establish the fixed four-contrast task; neural predictors and side-specific acquisition are prior results, not discoveries of this campaign.
- Bruijns et al., [Infinite hidden Markov models can dissect the complexities of learning](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41593-025-02130-x), published online30December2025, Nature Neuroscience29(2026),186–194; [diHMM code](https://github.com/SebastianBruijns/diHMM) `9a27fc9bc536ebd937a67c79731b64d247887403`. The primary text and preserved feasibility review establish the close descriptive learning-stage prior; the campaign does not rerun that large descriptive model or claim novelty for stages/history dependence.

Preserve this residual code-provenance gap; no zero-contrast external trials are generated.

## What this bounded review supports

The planned distinction is autonomous multi-session acquisition under a generated trainer and potential frozen transport across a bundled cohort/protocol shift. Existing work already predicts unfamiliar animals and future trials conditionally, describes stages/history dependence, compares curricula retrospectively and predicts individual acquisition from neural measurements. This campaign makes no exhaustive novelty claim and no unique synaptic, causal curriculum or classroom claim.
