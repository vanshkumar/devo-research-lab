## Fixed predictor
Fit all responding development trials200–599 with a hierarchical regularized logistic likelihood. Eight nuisance columns are intercept, four signed contrast-level indicators(.1,.25,.5,1), previous choice, centered later time(trial−399.5)/200, and sensory sign×time. Both models include condition×all nuisance and four early-calibration covariates×all nuisance. Calibration covariates are mean choice×signed stimulus on responding nonzero-contrast trials0–99 and100–199, mean first200response bias, and first200omission fraction. Center on development people in natural units. These are observable noisy summaries, not estimates of a latent sensory mechanism.

Both add causal feedback-side EWMAs with time constants2,8,32 trials and calibration×each history. Feedback-side evidence is own choice×correctness after a valid response; omission contributes zero information/decay. The alternative alone adds centered condition×each of three histories. Participant random intercept, sensory-sign and three history coefficients have independent Normal(0,.7²) residual priors in both accounts. Shared has63fixed coefficients, condition-dependent66. Fixed coefficient priorSD2, interceptSD3, calibration interactionsSD1, condition-historySD1. Nuisance condition coefficients haveSD2. No-feedback baseline retains previous choice and has48fixed coefficients with random intercept/sensory only; it is not a history-free agent.

Fit is a penalized joint mode using analytic gradients and L-BFGS, ftol1e-14, maximum1200iterations, up to3same-objective warm restarts, and mandatory maximum absolute gradient<.001. It is not a marginal likelihood or Bayes-factor calculation. Reserved-person prediction conditions on their first200 summaries, uses strictly past responses/feedback, and integrates fixed Gaussian residual variation with20-node Gauss–Hermite quadrature. Residual coefficients are not posterior-fitted to reserved later responses. No hidden probability, switch-offset or current target-side column enters the predictor.

## Recovery-informed interpretation gate
The final recovery uses100simulations for each of six actual-schedule populations: nuisance-only null, shared latent-skill/nonstationary null, slow-history+5pp,+10pp,−5pp, and fast-to-slow kernel redistribution+5pp. Injections were calibrated on independent prior-predictive draws. Nested effect intervals use20outer×50inner bootstrap fits per cell, except negative5pp10outer×50inner. Both-sign and kernel-shape recovery are required. The generic simulated taught high-contrast behavior is noisier than development data; rates are conditional stress tests, not universal empirical power.

Independent30-rep calibration diagnostics found that observable early-summary variants did not fix negative-effect attenuation. Oracle skill removed shared-law proxy bias but left symmetric shrinkage; widening the group-history prior improved that bias. Thus **the equivalence gate fails for an uncorrected model interval**, even if the real interval fits inside±.05. Preserve the original margin and report the empirical prediction and measured discrimination limits. Do not subtract a simulated mean bias from the real effect. A predictive association can be established by withheld score gain; it does not identify a direct teaching mechanism.
