# Frozen scope and split protocol — 2026-10-28

Status: written after input-only inspection and split, before any full trial response analysis. Modeling implementation/recovery refinement is allowed using development participants and simulated outcomes only, with dated amendments. A final hash freeze is required before opening validation responses. This is a timestamped prospective analysis plan within this investigation, not externally registered preregistration.

## Data and estimand
All 80 publicly released participants (600 trials each) are eligible regardless of accuracy. Teaching bundles rules, examples and five practice trials; condition is not randomized. The target is predictive association within this session, not a causal instruction effect or a neural mechanism.

The later interval is zero-based trials 200–599 (human-readable trials 201–600). Primary evaluation includes responded trials at contrast 0 or .1, at offsets 0–29 after a .2↔.8 hidden-probability switch beginning within that interval. Unbiased→biased transitions are excluded. If windows overlap, use the most recent switch. Omissions are excluded from binary choice scores and reported by condition/window; they do not reveal a feedback target. No participant is excluded for weak performance. Finite coding inconsistencies will be reported and cause an explicit data-handling amendment rather than silent recoding.

Primary score: each participant's mean Bernoulli log score difference, condition-dependent minus shared model, averaged equally across validation participants. Positive favors condition information. Report separate unseen-schedule (25 people, 3 complete schedules) and familiar-schedule/unseen-person (14 people, 7 schedules) results. Also report Brier differences, no-history baseline, per-person and per-schedule scores, and full later-trial scores as secondary outcomes. No oracle block label enters the primary predictor.

## Protected validation

No group parameters, thresholds, windows or hyperparameters may be selected using their outcomes.

## Primary predictive accounts
Chronological EWMA histories at time constants2,8,32 trials are computed from past feedback-reconstructed signed target side, with zero information/decay on omission. Features are read before the current response/feedback. Hidden probabilities only define windows and align descriptive curves. Past choice is an additional response-persistence nuisance, not a separately identified reward mechanism.

Both models receive the same condition-specific sensory, response-bias, lapse/saturation and time-trend flexibility; first200 calibration metrics predict later nuisance and history weights under a common relationship; participant residual coefficients have the same distribution/penalties in each model. The alternative adds only condition×history terms. Current signed contrast is represented separately at each nonzero contrast level to avoid forcing a single psychometric slope or fixed ceiling. This accommodates sensory/lapse patterns phenomenologically; it does not identify a biological lapse rate. Random intercept, sensory and history weights allow heterogeneous participants. The primary is a regularized hierarchical logistic prediction model, not a Bayes-factor or latent-mechanism test.

New-person prediction integrates residual random coefficients using the same Gaussian prior and quadrature in both models, conditioned on first200 calibration metrics. It does not fit population parameters from reserved responses. Causal histories use past observed response/feedback. Consequential implementation, penalty, standardization and uncertainty details will be hashed at the final freeze after synthetic recovery and development checks.

## Observable adaptation and practical scale
Report old-majority-aligned response probabilities for bins0–4,5–9,10–19,20–29 after later switches, including the10 trials before each switch as descriptive context. Report zero-contrast separately and all-low-contrast primary curves. Curve denominators and participant/schedule coverage must accompany estimates.

The prespecified practical threshold is **5 percentage points** in the mean old-context-aligned choice probability over offsets0–19 at zero current contrast, when changing only the condition-dependent history terms while holding nuisance/calibration covariates and evidence histories fixed. This is an observable model-standardized association, not a raw condition accuracy contrast. A signed mean can miss crossing trajectories; additionally report binwise and RMS contrasts. A nonsignificant score difference alone is not equivalence. An informative shared-adaptation claim requires a bound inside±.05, adequate simulated recovery at that scale and compatible withheld scores. If recovery or uncertainty does not support that inference, quantify the surviving ambiguity rather than relabeling null significance as equivalence.

## Recovery and controls before validation
Simulate actual schedules and condition allocation with participant heterogeneity and schedule effects. Test shared dynamics with different sensory/lapse, bias, early acquisition and their common association with history; shared nonstationary dynamics; condition-dependent history amplitudes and time scales that produce≥.05 standardized contrasts. Run the full development-fit/validation-predict process. Record false improvements, effect bias, power, precision and failure regimes. Fix a defensible recovery count based on measured local cost, without inspecting validation outcomes. Failed first recovery triggers measurement/model diagnosis and visible amendment, not immediate retreat.

## Uncertainty and challenges
People are biological units. Use participant bootstrap stratified by validation stratum and condition; report an additional schedule-resampling or leave-schedule-out assessment, explicitly noting only three unseen schedules. Refit development parameters in bootstrap for the standardized adaptation-effect interval. Check the assignment exceptions006/009/010, session order/protocol evidence, stimulus-schedule imbalance, omission coverage, zero contrast, remaining sensory saturation and motor latency, and early-acquisition carryover. Prespecify sensitivity variants at final freeze. An independent source-byte extraction and scoring check plus adversarial code/scientific audit are required.

## Decision rules
Persistent predictive value requires positive subject-level mean log-score improvement with uncertainty, consistent unseen-schedule evidence, an interpretable observable contrast, and surviving nuisance/control checks. Shared adaptation receives bounded support only under the recovery/equivalence conditions above. Otherwise report the measured discrimination limit and the additional independent observation required. No claims about long-term retention, children, education or neural storage.
