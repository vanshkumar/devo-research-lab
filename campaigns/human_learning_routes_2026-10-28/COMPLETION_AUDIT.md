## What closes the scientific question for this campaign
The requested positive result was not found. The primary shared account transfers as a predictor, and every frozen nuisance challenge agrees that additional condition-history weights supply no demonstrated gain. The empirical effect interval crosses the meaningful boundary, and known-effect recovery shows asymmetric attenuation and poor five-point discrimination under both attractive and repulsive history regimes. This is a quantified limit of the tested procedure, not a nonsignificant p-value renamed equivalence or a claim that all possible analyses must fail.

The exact further information needed is independent stable sensory/lapse measurement and new balanced stimulus schedules, analyzed with a validated joint measurement model; mere extra trials, cursor endpoints or accuracy matching are insufficient. A sufficiently powered and specific collection design has not yet been established. That uncertainty is the scientific result, not unfinished execution of the frozen study.
