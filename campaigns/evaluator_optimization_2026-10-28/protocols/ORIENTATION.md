# Research-owner choice and first discriminating test

Declared 2026-10-28, before any new plant outcomes. The lab's eight completed campaigns and both sensory preflights are development evidence. Historical recommendations do not bind this choice. Independent bounded reviews are saved in `campaigns/research_agenda_2026-10-28/reviews/`.

## Choice

Preserve the prospective mouse forecast result as the lab's strongest empirical account. Do not fit another repair or open the external animals. The older song, atlas, motor and human studies do not currently supply the defining measurements needed for their strongest biological questions. Select one controlled study of **whether a locally learned sensory evaluator supplies useful complete motor optimization through unfamiliar reachable plants**. The scientific uncertainty is teaching utility, rather than another passive response or gain conflict. This is a computational sufficiency/limitation result, not biological validation or a priority claim.

## Hypothesis and alternative

Primary hypothesis: after 35 tutor renditions, the published cancellation circuit provides substantial target-specific learning through independently fixed nonlinear motor plants: its joint shape/gain actor achieves at least half the true-loss improvement of a matched oracle on average across plants, and materially beats an untutored circuit. This positive hypothesis deliberately permits the possibility that the earlier gain conflict is escaped during complete learning.

Observable endpoint: terminal true feature MSE divided by each plant's initial MSE, after the fixed scalar-feedback budget. Primary utility criterion is mean learned improvement >= .5 * mean oracle improvement, with paired whole-plant bootstrap uncertainty. Also require learned improvement to exceed untutored improvement with a 95% paired interval above zero. These are campaign investment criteria, not universal biological thresholds.

Strongest alternatives: insufficient actor optimization; a finite-tutoring transient; disappearance of target-specific information due to normalization; and an amplitude-axis reward bias. A successful oracle is necessary. Longer tutoring (140 renditions), longer motor optimization, and fixed-gain ablation are prespecified secondary discriminations. Fixed gain removes a legitimate action axis and is not a task-preserving repair.

## Evidence and experiment

Use the unchanged pinned Gong 600E/150I network and plasticity, source-like 15-HVC fixed clock and six 100-ms contexts, zero circuit noise. Reuse source code and initialization helpers, never the old consumed plant/state. Each new whole plant draws six separate 100-channel rectified affine maps from a **shared four-dimensional motor-program action**, independently of its target. Each context is centered and standardized to SD2, as in the earlier feature assay; a common nonnegative gain multiplies signed auditory drive after normalization. Thus this is an explicitly low-dimensional motor manifold across six supplied contexts, not independent syllable policies, a vocal organ or sound pressure. The target is generated by a later action draw at gain1, guaranteeing exact reachability. Initial action is an independent draw, with gain1.

Draw plant parameters first, then target and initial action, then circuit. Plant basis N(0,1)/sqrt(4), bias N(-.4,.2); target and initial actions uniform[-1,1]^4. Search action bounds[-2,2]^4, gain[0,2]. No rejection based on performance or target distance. The learned actor receives only its selected scalar circuit cost per query; it never sees target action, target features, true loss, derivatives through the plant or a tutor-derived motor dictionary. Target tutoring and the explicit clock are the learned actor's target-information budget. True-loss scoring is offline and inaccessible to its update. The oracle is intentionally privileged.

Use deterministic coordinate pattern search: evaluate all +/- coordinate proposals at a common step; accept the lowest cost if it improves the current reward, otherwise halve the step. Initial step .4, minimum step .00625. Repeated or clipped proposals still consume budget. Fixed-gain search receives repeated center queries in its omitted gain slots, matching the joint search's total query count. No line searches or uncounted feedback to the actor. All arms start from the same target-blind action, use the same proposal rule, bounds, initial step and rounds. Their subsequent proposals differ because their reward histories differ. This is favorable derivative-free optimization, not a biologically realistic actor-critic algorithm.

First discriminating experiment: three development plants (IDs0,1,2; seed31000+ID), all five arms (oracle joint, learned35 joint, learned35 fixed gain, learned140 joint, untutored joint), initially32 rounds/321 queries each. Inspect complete true-loss trajectories. If the oracle does not achieve >=75% mean true-loss reduction, extend development to64 rounds before any new held-out plant, once. If the oracle still fails, stop this assay. No model or plant-family repair tournament.

If oracle success and learned useful behavior or meaningful persistent deficit make the assay informative, freeze one held-out protocol on12 new entire plants (IDs100..111; seeds31100..31111), all five arms,64 rounds/641 queries. At most four simultaneous processes, with BLAS threads limited to1; training and probing remain the source implementation. Expected total compute: roughly1 minute per trained circuit plus .12s/query; four circuit-scored arms *641 queries *12 = ~62 CPU minutes plus training, around20 wall minutes at four processes. Peak per-process matrices are modest; no new dependencies, paid resources or empirical data.

Secondary challenges declared now: extend learned35 and oracle joint to128 rounds using the saved actor state (additional640 queries), and compare35 versus140 tutoring at64 rounds. Use exact-target finite differences (h=.025) and one normalized .05-norm downhill step only as a diagnostic; target actions do not initialize primary actors. Record true-loss changes and circuit-cost changes separately. Any target-gradient/endpoint association is exploratory unless frozen after development. No generic reward correction is planned.

## Stopping and verification

Continue only to a bounded account of meaningful held-out learning or a reproducible limitation that survives the successful oracle and tutoring/budget challenges. Stop after those tests and an adversarial check. If the outcome merely reproduces a generic bad gradient without a learning consequence, consolidate and stop without claiming new science. Verify generated target reachability, actor information/query accounting, source hashes, independently recomputed feature loss, and at least selected decisive circuit scores through an independently written dynamics implementation. Exact replay alone is software verification; whole-plant held-out learning and oracle controls are the scientific test.

No protected empirical outcomes are authorized or needed. All20 external Pan-Vazquez prefixes/outcomes remain sealed; old exposure ledgers remain authoritative. No historical report is overwritten.
