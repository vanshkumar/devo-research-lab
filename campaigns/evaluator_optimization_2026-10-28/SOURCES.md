# Primary sources and scope

Inspected2026-10-28. See the independent bounded prior review in `../research_agenda_2026-10-28/reviews/evaluator_prior.md` and original source manifest `../../sources/sensory_evaluator_feasibility_2026-10-28/MANIFEST.json`.

- [Gong, Duarte, Mooney & Pearson, reviewed preprint v1,7 September2026](https://elifesciences.org/reviewed-preprints/111771). Figure5 already studies input gain, cancellation geometry and movement of the response minimum toward the tutor. Figure6/S12 already demonstrate simple scalar-reward motor learning and memory/landscape interventions. Their incomplete-convergence explanation motivates the140-rendition control. This campaign cannot claim predictive cancellation, gain landscapes or learned internal rewards as new ideas.
- [Pinned source code,8309db5b1e2abb0d937fec7a1f9d434b06a9bd7c](https://github.com/gongziyida/vocal-error-network/tree/8309db5b1e2abb0d937fec7a1f9d434b06a9bd7c), especially `src/models.py`, `src/train_funcs.py`, `src/RL.py`, `further_analysis/RL_syl_generation.ipynb`. The inspected notebook derives its motor dictionary from adult target spectrograms and supplies syllable timing. The present source network/plasticity are unchanged, but the synthetic motor map is independently sampled. Its four shared program coordinates across six contexts are an explicit restricted family, not a replication of the published actor or natural vocal biomechanics.
- [Fiete, Fee & Seung2007](https://journals.physiology.org/doi/full/10.1152/jn.01311.2006). Internal tutor comparison plus perturbation-driven motor learning is established prior. Our deterministic scalar-feedback search tests reward utility favorably, without a claim of neural realism or sample-efficient biological learning.
- [Gao, Schulman & Hilton2023](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v202/gao23h.html). True-objective degradation under learned reward optimization is established. Another bad proxy gradient would be insufficient contribution; the empirical object here is full learning through independently held-out reachable plants and the tutoring-versus-search discrimination.
- [Skalse et al.,v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.13085v2). General reward conflict results do not determine useful behavior under this particular constrained plant/actor. Actual optimization controls are needed.

No exhaustive priority claim is made. No new natural songs, neural recordings, protected animal observations or additional source datasets were acquired. Reused previous preflights are consumed development material, not held-out replications.
