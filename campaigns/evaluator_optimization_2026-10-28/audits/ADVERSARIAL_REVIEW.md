## Strongest falsification argument

The strongest objection to a successful result is that this assay is unusually favorable: an exact reachable target lies on a smooth, shared four-dimensional motor manifold; six contexts, an accurate clock, signed normalized drive and noiseless reset-based scalar probes are supplied; a flexible global/local search makes hundreds of deterministic queries. A newly tutored circuit is supplied for every plant. Therefore success would show that target-specific information stored by this circuit can guide this particular complete optimization, not that a single learned evaluator transfers across plants, that a biological actor can exploit it, or that the source mechanism learns natural vocal behavior. The untutored and context-shuffled arms are crucial because the simple motor manifold and generic attenuation could otherwise produce apparent improvement without a useful target memory.

This objection would defeat a broad biological or cross-plant-transfer claim. It would not defeat the declared computational sufficiency claim if the completed held-out mean utility and both correct-tutor control contrasts pass, absolute endpoints show meaningful fidelity, and independent loss/query/dynamics audits agree. Conversely, a poor learned result cannot be attributed to reward alone unless the matched oracle succeeds; even a successful oracle does not certify the learned reward's global optimum.

## Interpretation constraints

1. **Mean improvement is not fidelity.** An initially poor program can improve by attenuating toward absent auditory drive. Report absolute terminal MSE, final gain and the frozenMSE<=.4 count alongside normalized improvement. The nominal target energy is4 only when every target context has nonzero normalized variance; any zero-variance case must retain its actual feature energy and declared edge handling.
2. **Shuffling is an association control.** A cyclic permutation preserves marginal target patterns but usually produces an unreachable six-context combination for the shared motor program. This appropriately tests the utility of the correct target/context association. It is not a fair alternative target-acquisition task or a mechanism-identification test.
3. **The oracle is a finite-budget reference.** Report every plant's oracle endpoint, including failures. The held-out mean75% gate can coexist with individual poor oracles. Their corresponding learner failures cannot by themselves identify a reward defect, and the128-round challenge remains finite search.
4. **The half-oracle rule is initially a point criterion.** The ratio/contrast interval must be reported. Passing the point rule alone does not establish a population lower bound of half-oracle utility. Ordinary paired percentile intervals atN12 are descriptive under this generator; they do not confer broad biological generality or a familywise guarantee for secondary findings.
5. **Longer tutoring is an exposure sensitivity.** Literal140 does not imply convergence or a better teacher. Development diagnostics contain high finite exact-target responses and sharp changes after small perturbations. Agreement between two fixed-step implementations would not distinguish a nonlinear response transition, observation-window dependence, oscillations or a discretization effect.
6. **Target-start diagnostics use privileged information separately.** A directly evaluated lower-cost incorrect output falsifies exact-target score minimality at the sampled scale. Centered differences withh=.025 are finite-scale directions; they do not establish a smooth infinitesimal derivative or predict the basin reached from ordinary starts. No such diagnostic information enters the primary actor.
7. **Local reward errors and useful learning may coexist.** Reward-improving/true-worsening accepted steps or target-local drift do not refute substantial terminal utility. A strong terminal result does not establish globally correct reward ordering, exact recovery or global convergence.
