# Sources and provenance

Original data bytes are reused from ../../sources/learning_intervention_feasibility_2026-10-28/. None was overwritten. provenance/source_manifest.json records original commit, Git blob, SHA256, size and URL. Original source manifest/audit are preserved. Independent byte-exact code snapshots and verification are in audits/source_measurement/code/ and audit_results.json.

- Tsay et al.2022, *Interactions between sensory prediction error and task error during implicit motor learning*, [PLOS Computational Biology](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pcbi.1010005). Primary endpoint/timing, participant instructions, Experiment2 geometry and six-model background. Author [target_jump repository](https://github.com/xiaotsay2015/target_jump), commit049a846a4207a8a047a3f336bcf63ce16e550ddf. TE_SPE_data_5Feb2022 and ControlJump_data_5Feb2022 are paper Experiment2 A/B despite legacy Exp labels. Pinned repository has no acquisition, export preprocessing or randomization implementation. Actual TN order supports180-trial balancing, not described24-trial balancing.
- Al-Fawakhiri et al.2023, *Exploring the role of task success in implicit motor adaptation*, [Journal of Neurophysiology](https://doi.org/10.1152/jn.00061.2023). [Author repository](https://github.com/kimoli/TaskSuccessImplicitAdapt), commit40ae8aa7c9302ae12c7973fab4748d003e8fc9db. Figure3 is selected transfer; Figure4 outcomes remain reserved unless specifically released. Main outcomes are HandError at target-distance endpoint. No randomized zero-clamp main trials. See audit for flicker0.01 and Figure4 sign conventions and target-trigger timing inconsistency.
- Kim, Parvin & Ivry2019, [*The influence of task outcome on implicit motor learning*](https://elifesciences.org/articles/39882). Prior learning/retention and multiple-process comparisons, parameter correlations. Gain-versus-retention is not novel.
- Tsay et al.2022, [PReMo](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9377801/), equations12–13. Motivation for goal-minus-perceived-hand updates. The report's exact retention/perceived-error equivalence is an independently constructed witness, not a fitted reproduction of PReMo.
- [Official R operator precedence](https://stat.ethz.ch/R-manual/R-devel/library/base/html/Syntax.html) establishes the literal external author-script index i-2:i+2 differs from (i-2):(i+2). We do not inherit that masking algorithm for the primary or transport endpoint.

Full inspection scope, precise pinned code lines, measured transformation discrepancies and bounded prior-work search are in audits/source_measurement/AUDIT.md. Published aggregates were known before the split; primary raw carryover outcomes were not. No claim of exhaustive novelty, reward isolation, acquisition-raw endpoint provenance or exact randomization inference is made.
