All outcome p-values remain source reports, not recalculated independent evidence. Raw datasets are on request; no contact attempted. Figure3 controls are reused, not independent replications. Playback controls pooled three subgroups of two birds (GFP, no virus with illumination, playback only); inability to detect subgroup difference with two birds does not establish equivalence.

Model mapping: acquisition gate a=g_DA*g_pathway. DA blockade during tutoring can set g_DA=0 in reference, motor-scaffold, and shared-code models. Blocking after tutoring leaves stored state unchanged in all three. This mapping is supported at the input/gate level; it does not localize the durable state or prove a particular downstream loss function. Similarity is a composite SAP percent-similarity score averaged over≥10 comparisons per bird, not a calibrated duration error, so this campaign does not silently use similarity as milliseconds or fit a duration simulator to it.
