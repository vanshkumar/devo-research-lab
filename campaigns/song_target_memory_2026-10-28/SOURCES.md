## Contrary and adjacent evidence

The [prior-work audit](audits/prior_work/PRIOR_AUDIT.md) gives exact claims and inspection limits:

- [Hamaguchi, Tanaka & Mooney2016](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4975959/): full indexed primary Results/Figs4–5; Uva contributes to timing, limiting a uniquely local HVC-clock interpretation.
- [Roberts et al.2012](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3458123/): full indexed primary Results/Methods, tutoring-contingent versus later disruption controls; acquisition necessity is not unique storage evidence.
- [Otchy et al.2015](https://www.nature.com/articles/nature16442): primary abstract and extended controls; acute/chronic NIf effects support auditing permissive downstream changes.
- [Mackevicius et al.2023](https://elifesciences.org/articles/77262): primary Results/Discussion, four tracked learner birds and pre-tutoring-only sequence extraction; pre-existing sequences can be recruited into new syllables. Declared Dryad archive was not downloaded in this campaign.
- [Gobes & Bolhuis2007](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17433683/): abstract only; tutor recognition and song production dissociate.
- [Yu et al.2023](https://escholarship.org/content/qt704647k4/qt704647k4.pdf): PDF pp1–3; vocalizer recognition is a different endpoint from imitative learning.
- [Tarnaski et al.2025](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304394025002629): abstract/introduction and public methods excerpt only; preference cannot simply substitute for imitation. Full article uninspected.

The2026 Fee seminar was an initial lead, superseded by identified2023 conference abstracts and2024 thesis metadata. It supplies no quantitative validation here. No claim of exhaustive literature coverage or novelty rests on a failed search.

## Acquisition and transformations

All acquisition used normal public access: pages, an ordinary browser download, author-hosted PDF, and supported archive-member HTTP206 reads. No challenge was bypassed; throttling was honored. Original MATLAB code remains unmodified. Derived outputs include text extraction, manually selected/digitized source figure coordinates, exhaustive assignment bounds, a declared port, and controlled synthetic observations.

RESOURCE_ACCOUNTING.json counts27,907,414 retained/known transferred bytes plus explicitly unmetered failures, search/browser responses and overhead. This subtotal is not total network usage. The full suite took904.913 summed child-process seconds; it is local computation, not network or paid compute. ENVIRONMENT.json and requirements-lock.txt pin execution dependencies. INPUTS.json pins evidence and runnable analyses; CACHED_MODELS.json fingerprints numerical arrays and JSON excluding elapsed-time telemetry. Review snapshots may predate documented final wording fixes; original reviews are preserved.
