## Scientific bet

Can available interventions distinguish a stored reference used to judge future song from exposure-induced changes to motor timing that constrain what the bird can learn? These are provisional operational alternatives, not assumed to be cleanly separate in the brain. Their apparent distinction may collapse when formalized; establishing that would be informative. A shared sensory–motor representation is a third possibility, not excluded by phrasing a binary question.

The most useful entry point is target-writing and conflicting-tutoring evidence. The prior campaign concentrated on evaluative signals. This question tests the acquisition of what later counts as success, directly relevant to learned goals and innate constraints.
